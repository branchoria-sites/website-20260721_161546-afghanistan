## Introduction

[Cape Verde]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-cape-verdes-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO timeline is unusually short and thinly documented. Unlike countries with extensive military archives or decades of recurring reports, the public record for Cape Verde consists mainly of a handful of internet-era sightings between 2011 and the mid-2020s. Most are supported only by witness testimony, photographs or videos uploaded online, with little or no corroborating [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) such as radar records, aviation data, astronomical analysis or official investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latest-ufo-sightings.net/tag/cape-verde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latest-ufo-sightings.net">[Latest UFO Sightings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latest-ufo-sightings.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span></span></span>

## Which UFO sightings were actually reported?

### The 2011 São Vicente lights

The earliest widely circulated modern Cape Verde case appeared in June 2011, when a UFO website published video said to show two bright linked lights and additional luminous activity over São Vicente. The footage was presented as having been recorded on 5 June 2011, but little supporting information accompanied the publication. The original recording conditions, camera specifications, witness interviews and precise observation location were not documented publicly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latest-ufo-sightings.net/tag/cape-verde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latest-ufo-sightings.net">[Latest UFO Sightings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latest-ufo-sightings.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span></span></span>

Because the material exists primarily through reposted online video, later researchers have been unable to establish several key facts that would normally be examined in a credible investigation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* whether the published video is the original recording or a compressed copy;
* whether multiple independent witnesses observed the same event;
* whether aircraft movements, satellites or astronomical objects were checked;
* whether any local authority documented the incident.

</div>

As a result, the São Vicente lights remain genuinely unidentified only in the limited sense that the available evidence is insufficient for a confident explanation. The lack of documentation also prevents them from becoming a strong evidential case.

### The 2012 Praia observation

Although often overlooked in summaries of Cape Verde reports, the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC) contains a sighting submitted almost immediately after it allegedly occurred during a holiday on Santiago Island. According to the report, two witnesses observed what first appeared to be a bright star behaving unusually, followed by orange flashing lights and a separate extremely bright object moving rapidly across the sky during the night of 30–31 May 2012 near Praia. The witnesses attempted to photograph the display but reported that their camera could not capture it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=89322" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 89322June 5, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 5, 2012</span></span></span>

Several features distinguish this report from many internet sightings:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* it was filed within days rather than years;
* it includes a specific island, approximate date and observational narrative;
* it acknowledges unsuccessful attempts to obtain photographic evidence.

</div>

However, important limitations remain. No photographs accompanied the report, there is no independent confirmation from other observers, and no published investigation compared the observations with astronomical events, aircraft or satellites. Consequently, the case remains anecdotal despite its relatively prompt reporting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=89322" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 89322June 5, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 5, 2012</span></span></span>

### The 2013 tourist video

A separate internet-era claim concerns footage reportedly recorded by a tourist in Cape Verde during 2013. Like the earlier São Vicente case, the video circulated mainly through UFO-focused websites rather than through news organisations or scientific investigators.

Public descriptions of the recording remain sparse. Exact filming conditions, original video files and independent image analysis have not been made available, making it impossible to determine whether the object represented an aircraft, balloon, optical effect or something genuinely anomalous. The limited documentation means the case adds another entry to the chronology but contributes little robust evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latest-ufo-sightings.net/tag/cape-verde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latest-ufo-sightings.net">[Latest UFO Sightings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latest-ufo-sightings.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span></span></span>

### The 2017 holiday photographs

One of the better-known Cape Verde claims emerged in October 2017, when a British couple stated that they discovered a metallic blue object only after reviewing photographs taken during their holiday near the Cape Verde coast. Unlike a conventional sighting, the observers did not report watching the object move through the sky while taking the photographs. Instead, the alleged anomaly was noticed retrospectively during image review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latest-ufo-sightings.net/tag/cape-verde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latest-ufo-sightings.net">[Latest UFO Sightings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latest-ufo-sightings.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span></span></span>

This distinction is significant because retrospective photographic discoveries introduce several possibilities that are difficult to exclude without the original image files, including:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* birds captured during a single exposure;
* insects passing close to the lens;
* motion blur;
* lens reflections;
* digital image artefacts.

</div>

No publicly available forensic examination of the original image metadata or full-resolution files has demonstrated that these possibilities were systematically eliminated. Consequently, the photographs remain interesting but evidentially weak.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latest-ufo-sightings.net/tag/cape-verde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latest-ufo-sightings.net">[Latest UFO Sightings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latest-ufo-sightings.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Latest UFO Sightings Cape Verde Archives • Latest UFO Sightings</span></span></span>

### The delayed Atlantic maritime report

The most recent addition to the chronology is unusual because it was reported many years after the alleged event. A NUFORC entry submitted in 2025 describes an incident that reportedly occurred during a yacht voyage from Cape Verde towards Barbados in December 2012. According to the account, two bright white spherical objects approximately two to three metres in apparent diameter followed the vessel for several minutes after a tanker had passed before disappearing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=193255" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 193255...</span></span></span>

The report is difficult to evaluate because several important details remain uncertain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* it was submitted roughly thirteen years after the alleged observation;
* the exact position at sea is unknown;
* no supporting witnesses are identified;
* no navigational records or photographs accompany the account.

</div>

Although NUFORC categorises the sighting under Cabo Verde because the voyage originated there, the event itself occurred somewhere in the mid-Atlantic rather than near the islands. It therefore belongs only cautiously within a Cape Verde chronology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=193255" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 193255...</span></span></span>

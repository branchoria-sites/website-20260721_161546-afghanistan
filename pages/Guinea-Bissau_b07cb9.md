## Introduction

Guinea-Bissau has one of the sparsest documented UFO [records]({{ 'records-bb3688/' | relative_url }}) in West Africa. ## What UFO cases are recorded in Guinea-Bissau?

The clearest publicly indexed Guinea-Bissau UFO report concerns an event said to have occurred on 4 January 1967. ## Guinea-Bissau’s UFO record in perspective

Guinea-Bissau represents a broader pattern seen in many countries where UFO claims exist without extensive documentation. The available evidence supports the existence of at least one reported unusual sighting, but not the existence of confirmed unknown craft, extraterrestrial visits, or a hidden national investigation programme.

The most reliable picture is therefore one of limited but intriguing reports: a small number of claims, little official documentation, and no publicly verified evidence. Guinea-Bissau’s UFO history remains primarily a record of human observations rather than a demonstrated record of unexplained technology.

## Introduction

[Liberia]({{ 'what-do-we-really-know-about-liberias/' | relative_url }}) has no well-documented concentration of UFO reports linked to any particular airport or stretch of coastline. Nevertheless, if unusual aerial sightings were to be reported within the country, they would most plausibly emerge around its busiest aviation corridors, Atlantic shoreline, and the populated coastal belt surrounding Monrovia. These are the locations where more people regularly observe the sky, where aircraft movements are concentrated, and where unusual lights can sometimes be checked against known aviation and maritime activity rather than remaining unexplained.

The available evidence therefore supports a geographical assessment rather than a catalogue of famous incidents. Liberia's airports and coastlines are significant because they create environments where ordinary aircraft, ships, atmospheric conditions and celestial objects may be mistaken for something unfamiliar, while also offering the best opportunities for any genuine unidentified observation to be compared with flight operations and official aviation records.

## Why airports and coastlines dominate observation opportunities

Unlike many inland regions of Liberia, the country's principal airports and coastal settlements combine several factors that increase the likelihood of reports:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

## Monrovia and the Spriggs Payne airspace

James Spriggs Payne Airport lies only a few kilometres from central Monrovia, placing regular aircraft operations directly alongside Liberia's largest concentration of residents. The airport has a paved runway and serves domestic and government aviation, while Roberts International Airport handles most scheduled international traffic outside the capital.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lcaa.gov.lr/index.php/general/airports" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lcaa.gov.lr">[Liberia Civil Aviation Authority]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lcaa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Liberia Civil Aviation Authority Airports &#124; Liberia Civil Aviation Authority</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Liberia Civil Aviation Authority Airports &#124; Liberia Civil Aviation Authority</span></span></span>

Because Spriggs Payne sits within an urban environment, observers may see:<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spriggs_Payne_Airport" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Spriggs Payne Airport</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Spriggs Payne Airport</span></span></span>


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* aircraft approaching or departing at unusual angles;
* navigation lights viewed head-on;
* helicopters operating at relatively low altitude;
* lights appearing stationary because an aircraft is flying directly towards the observer.

</div>

One particularly relevant operational detail is that Liberia's air navigation system includes a visual flight rules (VFR) corridor running along the shoreline near Monrovia to separate light aircraft from instrument traffic approaching Roberts International Airport. Aircraft following this coastal route may appear unexpectedly low over the sea or coastline, especially during twilight or at night.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lcaa.gov.lr/general/air-space-management" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lcaa.gov.lr">[Liberia Civil Aviation Authority]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lcaa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on lcaa.gov.lr.</span></span></span>

From a UFO-investigation perspective, this is important because sightings near Monrovia can often be compared against known aviation procedures rather than being treated as isolated mysteries.

## Roberts International Airport and aviation-quality observations

Roberts International Airport (RIA), approximately 45 kilometres from Monrovia, is Liberia's primary international gateway and the centre of the country's controlled airspace. The Liberia Civil Aviation Authority manages air traffic services within the Roberts Flight Information Region and maintains published procedures for aircraft movement, navigation and search-and-rescue coordination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lcaa.gov.lr/general/air-space-management" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lcaa.gov.lr">[Liberia Civil Aviation Authority]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lcaa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on lcaa.gov.lr.</span></span></span>

This makes the Roberts area the location where any credible aerial observation would have the greatest chance of being evaluated against:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* scheduled commercial flights;
* air traffic control procedures;
* published flight corridors;
* meteorological information;
* aviation safety records.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://lcaa.gov.lr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lcaa.gov.lr">[lcaa.gov.lr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lcaa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Home &#124; Liberia Civil Aviation Authority</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Home &#124; Liberia Civil Aviation Authority</span></span></span>

</div>

Although Liberia primarily relies on procedural rather than radar-based air traffic control, controllers maintain detailed awareness of aircraft operating within the national terminal area through established communication and navigation systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lcaa.gov.lr/general/air-space-management" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lcaa.gov.lr">[Liberia Civil Aviation Authority]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lcaa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on lcaa.gov.lr.</span></span></span>

Importantly, no publicly available aviation records indicate that Roberts International Airport has generated a recognised UFO case supported by air traffic evidence or official investigation. Recent publicly documented airport incidents concern routine aviation safety matters, such as mechanical events, rather than unidentified aerial objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.micat.gov.lr/node/4432" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: micat.gov.lr">[Ministry of Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">micat.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ministry of InformationLAA Addresses Runway Incident Involving Ethiopian Airlines Flight ET 974 &#124; The Ministry of InformationJanuary 31...</span></span></span>

## Coastal viewing conditions and the Atlantic horizon

Liberia possesses approximately 580 kilometres of Atlantic coastline, providing long, unobstructed views over the ocean.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nic.gov.lr/pages/gov-support/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nic.gov.lr">[National Investment Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nic.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Investment Commission Government Support – Liberia &#124; NIC</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Investment Commission Government Support – Liberia &#124; NIC</span></span></span>

Open marine horizons create favourable conditions for observing distant lights, but they also increase opportunities for misidentification. Common sources include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* ships beyond the visible horizon;
* fishing vessels;
* navigation lights;
* offshore weather systems;
* aircraft approaching from over the Atlantic;
* bright planets or stars appearing low above the sea.

</div>

At night, atmospheric refraction and temperature differences above the ocean can make distant lights appear higher, brighter or more mobile than they actually are. Such effects are well documented in observational astronomy and light-propagation research over coastal waters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02508" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Light pollution offshore: zenithal sky glow measurements in the Mediterranean coastal watersMay 6, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 6, 2017</span></span></span>

The restoration of extensive lighting at the Freeport of Monrovia also illustrates how changing coastal infrastructure can alter the appearance of the night skyline. Bright industrial lighting, reflections on water and moving harbour equipment can all produce unfamiliar visual impressions for observers unfamiliar with port operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.npa.gov.lr/national-port-authority-restores-lighting-infrastructure-at-freeport-of-monrovia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: npa.gov.lr">[National Port Authority]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">npa.gov.lr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Port AuthorityNational Port Authority Restores Lighting Infrastructure at Freeport of MonroviaOctober 9, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 9, 2024</span></span></span>

## Interior darkness versus reporting opportunities

One interesting contrast within Liberia is that the country's interior may offer some of West Africa's darker night skies because of relatively low population density outside major settlements.

For astronomy, darker skies improve visibility of celestial objects.

For UFO reporting, however, darker skies do not automatically produce more reliable evidence. Several practical limitations remain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* far fewer potential witnesses;
* limited photographic documentation;
* reduced access to communications;
* fewer opportunities to compare observations with aviation activity.

</div>

As a result, a genuine unusual observation in remote Liberia would be less likely to generate a documented case than a comparable event near Monrovia or Roberts International Airport.

## Why most airport and coastal sightings have conventional explanations

The absence of documented Liberian UFO investigations does not mean unusual observations cannot occur. It does mean that the most likely explanations should be considered first.

Near airports, investigators would typically compare a report against:

* commercial and domestic flight movements;
* helicopter operations;
* approach and departure paths;
* airport lighting systems;
* meteorological conditions.

Along the coast, they would additionally examine:

* marine traffic;
* harbour activity;
* fishing fleets;
* atmospheric optics over the Atlantic;
* astronomical objects visible above the western horizon.

Only after these possibilities had been reasonably excluded would a report remain genuinely unidentified.

## What this means for Liberia's overall UFO record

The geographical pattern expected in Liberia differs from countries with extensive historical UFO archives. Rather than showing recognised "hotspots" based on repeated documented incidents, Liberia's most plausible sighting zones are simply the places where people most often observe the sky and where aviation activity is concentrated.

If a future well-documented Liberian case were to emerge, the strongest candidates would likely involve observations near Monrovia, Spriggs Payne Airport, Roberts International Airport or the Atlantic coastline, where independent witnesses, aviation authorities and environmental context provide the greatest opportunity to distinguish ordinary aircraft and maritime activity from a genuinely unexplained aerial observation. At present, however, no publicly available evidence demonstrates that these locations have produced a verified concentration of unexplained UFO events.

      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });

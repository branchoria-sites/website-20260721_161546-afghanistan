## Introduction

On the evening of 22 May 2026, thousands of people across Trinidad saw a brilliant white object moving across the western sky shortly after sunset. Videos spread rapidly on social media, prompting speculation that the object was a UFO, an unusual atmospheric event or, in local folklore, even a supernatural phenomenon. Within hours, however, the mystery had a clear and well-supported explanation: observers had witnessed the passage of SpaceX's Starship Flight 12, launched from Starbase, Texas, on a trajectory that carried the spacecraft across the Caribbean.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.guardian.co.tt/article/spacecraft-not-soucouyant-mystery-light-stirs-curiosity-across-trinidad-6.2.2591471.5d16fdcb1b" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: guardian.co.tt">[Trinidad Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">guardian.co.tt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Trinidad GuardianSpacecraft, not soucouyant: Mystery light stirs curiosity across Trinidad - Trinidad GuardianMay 24, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 24, 2026</span></span></span>

## What witnesses saw across Trinidad and the Caribbean

The object became visible between approximately 6:30 pm and 6:50 pm local time, just after sunset. Observers from Port of Spain, San Fernando and many other parts of Trinidad described a bright white or bluish object travelling steadily across the sky with a glowing halo or trailing plume. Unlike a typical aircraft, it appeared unusually luminous and seemed to move silently at high altitude.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.guardian.co.tt/article/spacecraft-not-soucouyant-mystery-light-stirs-curiosity-across-trinidad-6.2.2591471.5d16fdcb1b" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: guardian.co.tt">[Trinidad Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">guardian.co.tt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Trinidad GuardianSpacecraft, not soucouyant: Mystery light stirs curiosity across Trinidad - Trinidad GuardianMay 24, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 24, 2026</span></span></span>

## How launch timing and trajectory solved the mystery

The explanation proved unusually straightforward because the timing matched a widely documented space launch.

SpaceX launched Starship Flight 12 from Starbase, Texas, at approximately 5:30 pm Central Time on 22 May 2026. The mission marked the debut flight of the company's upgraded Starship V3 and Super Heavy V3 vehicles and followed an easterly suborbital trajectory toward the Indian Ocean. That flight path naturally carried the upper stage across the Caribbean, making it visible from Trinidad and Tobago during local twilight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Twelfth Flight Test...</span></span></span>

## Why twilight rocket plumes look so unusual

The visual appearance that surprised so many observers has a well-understood physical explanation.

At ground level, Trinidad was already entering dusk. High above the Earth, however, the spacecraft remained in direct sunlight. Exhaust gases released into the near-vacuum of the upper atmosphere expanded dramatically, producing an enormous illuminated plume that reflected sunlight long after sunset below.

This combination creates several visual effects that differ sharply from ordinary aircraft:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* the plume appears much larger than the spacecraft itself;
* sunlight makes the exhaust glow white or bluish;
* the object seems almost motionless because of its extreme altitude;
* the glowing cloud can change shape as gases expand in near-space; and
* observers unfamiliar with rocket launches often have no familiar object with which to compare it.

</div>

These optical effects have produced similar "mystery lights" following launches viewed from California, Florida, New Zealand and other regions, making the Trinidad event part of a broader class of well-understood aerospace observations rather than a unique anomaly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Twelfth Flight Test...</span></span></span>

## Why this became Trinidad's strongest identified UFO case

Unlike many historical UFO reports that remain based solely on eyewitness testimony, the May 2026 incident benefits from unusually strong corroboration.

The event was:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* witnessed by large numbers of independent observers;
* extensively recorded on video;
* precisely time-stamped;

      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);

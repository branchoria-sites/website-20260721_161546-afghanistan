## Introduction

The Kadima UFO wave is the best-known regional concentration of UFO reports in [Israel]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-israels-ufo/' | relative_url }}), but it is also one of the country's most disputed. Between roughly 1993 and 1998, residents of Kadima and neighbouring communities in the Sharon region reported repeated sightings of glowing aerial objects, alleged landing traces, humanoid encounters and unusual physical residues. Unlike a single dramatic incident, the Kadima stories evolved into a cluster of interconnected cases that reinforced one another through repeated witness testimony and the work of local UFO investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ynetnews.com/magazine/article/byerlvo1wl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ynetnews.com">[ynetglobal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ynetnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span></span></span>

What makes Kadima significant is not that it produced universally accepted evidence of extraordinary phenomena. Rather, it illustrates how a local wave can become a lasting UFO legend despite fragmented documentation and persistent disputes over the quality of the underlying evidence. Many of the most striking claims continue to circulate, but the original records, laboratory documentation and independently verifiable physical evidence remain incomplete or unavailable, leaving the wave in an unresolved rather than confirmed category.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ynetnews.com/magazine/article/byerlvo1wl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ynetnews.com">[ynetglobal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ynetnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span></span></span>

## The 1990s Sightings and Witness Stories

Much of the Kadima narrative revolves around recurring witness testimony collected by Israeli UFO investigators during the mid-1990s.

Among the most frequently cited witnesses is Tziporet Carmel, who described encountering a brightly illuminated object near her property together with a tall humanoid figure. She later expanded the account in a published memoir and became one of the defining figures in Kadima UFO literature. According to later retellings, the encounter was followed by the discovery of unusual circular marks in the surrounding field.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ynetnews.com/magazine/article/byerlvo1wl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ynetnews.com">[ynetglobal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ynetnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span></span></span>

Other residents reported:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* hovering lights moving silently;
* objects changing colour or intensity;
* repeated appearances over familiar locations;
* similar observations made independently by neighbours.

</div>

Supporters argue that the number of independent witnesses distinguishes Kadima from many one-off UFO claims. However, many reports were documented only after discussion within local UFO circles, making it difficult to determine how much testimony was influenced by prior publicity or shared expectations. Few original contemporaneous witness statements are publicly available.

Another complication is chronology. ## Ground Traces, Humanoids and Laboratory Claims

The most controversial aspect of the Kadima wave concerns alleged physical evidence.

### Landing circles

Several locations reportedly displayed circular ground depressions or scorched vegetation after UFO sightings. Some circles were described as measuring several metres across and became focal points for investigators who argued they represented landing sites rather than ordinary agricultural damage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ynetnews.com/magazine/article/byerlvo1wl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ynetnews.com">[ynetglobal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ynetnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span></span></span>

Unlike well-documented forensic investigations, however, these sites were not preserved under controlled conditions. Publicly available records generally lack:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* detailed mapping;
* continuous photographic documentation;
* independent surveying;
* documented chain of custody for collected samples.

</div>

Without those safeguards, later investigators cannot reliably determine whether the reported traces resulted from unusual activity, natural causes or later disturbance.

### Material samples

Perhaps the most repeated claim involves recovered material allegedly found within one landing circle.

Some investigators have stated that laboratory analysis identified highly pure silicon and other unusual substances. Other accounts describe cadmium-containing residues associated with additional sites. These findings are frequently cited within UFO literature as evidence that something physically unusual occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ynetnews.com/magazine/article/byerlvo1wl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ynetnews.com">[ynetglobal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ynetnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ynetglobal Israel&#x27;s UFO hotspot: town draws believers chasing alien encounters</span></span></span>

Critical information is generally absent from publicly available documentation, including:

* analytical methods;
* control samples;
* isotopic testing capable of determining origin;

### Humanoid encounters

Reports of tall humanoid beings became another defining element of the Kadima wave. Descriptions often involved figures substantially taller than average humans, wearing metallic or unusual clothing and communicating little or not at all.

These accounts remain among the weakest forms of evidence from an investigative standpoint because they rely almost entirely on personal testimony. No verified photographs, medical documentation or independently recorded physical evidence has entered the public record to corroborate the encounters.

## Why the Evidence Remains Contested

Kadima occupies an unusual position within Israeli UFO history because both believers and sceptics agree that numerous reports existed, while disagreeing sharply over what those reports demonstrate.

Supporters emphasise several factors:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* multiple witnesses over several years;
* recurring locations;
* alleged physical traces;
* consistency between many witness narratives;

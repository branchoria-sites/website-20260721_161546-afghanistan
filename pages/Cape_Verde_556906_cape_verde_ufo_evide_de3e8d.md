## Introduction

The strongest conclusion that can be drawn from the available evidence is that [Cape Verde]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-cape-verdes-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s reported UFO incidents remain **unidentified because the evidence is limited, not because it demonstrates an extraordinary phenomenon**. The publicly available material confirms that people photographed or reported unusual lights and objects, but it does not establish their speed, size, distance, flight characteristics or origin. In every known Cape Verde case, crucial information—such as original image files, multiple witness statements, radar records, air traffic data or independent forensic analysis—is either unavailable or has never been published.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/explainers/common-ufo-misidentifications" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[UFO Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ArchiveCommon UFO misidentifications: drones, satellites, balloons, aircraft, planets, and camera artifacts &#124; Global UFO ArchiveJune...</span></span></span>

## Why Camera Effects and Nearby Objects Can Be Misleading

One reason the evidence remains weak is that photographs and videos frequently remove the information needed to judge distance, scale and motion.

A bright light against a dark sky provides almost no visual reference points. Without knowing how far away the object is, it is impossible to calculate its actual speed or size. A nearby insect, bird or balloon can appear similar to a much larger distant object when viewed through a single camera lens.

Modern digital cameras introduce additional uncertainty through:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* digital zoom;
* image stabilisation;
* motion blur;
* autofocus hunting;
* sensor blooming around bright lights;
* video compression;
* lens reflections and internal optical artefacts.

</div>

Each of these effects can distort the appearance of lights or reflective objects, especially in low-light conditions. Investigators therefore place great importance on obtaining original, uncompressed files rather than videos repeatedly uploaded to social media, where compression permanently removes detail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/explainers/common-ufo-misidentifications" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[UFO Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ArchiveCommon UFO misidentifications: drones, satellites, balloons, aircraft, planets, and camera artifacts &#124; Global UFO ArchiveJune...</span></span></span>

Cape Verde's reported material generally survives only in compressed online form, making independent forensic examination extremely difficult.

## Why "Unresolved" Does Not Mean "Unexplainable"

A common misunderstanding in UFO discussions is that if no explanation has been proven, then an extraordinary explanation becomes more likely.

That is not how evidence is evaluated.

An incident can remain unresolved simply because too much information has been lost. Investigators cannot confidently identify an aircraft, balloon or astronomical object if they lack the original recording, exact viewing direction, weather conditions, observation time or witness interviews.

Cape Verde's reported cases fit this pattern.

Rather than containing evidence that rules out conventional explanations, they usually contain **too little evidence to test any explanation properly**. The absence of a definitive solution therefore reflects incomplete data rather than positive evidence for an exotic phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/explainers/common-ufo-misidentifications" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[UFO Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ArchiveCommon UFO misidentifications: drones, satellites, balloons, aircraft, planets, and camera artifacts &#124; Global UFO ArchiveJune...</span></span></span>

This distinction is especially important when comparing Cape Verde with countries that possess military sensor records, official investigation files or multiple independent observations. Cape Verde has no publicly available archive of comparable quality.

## What Is Missing From the Strongest Cases?

Strong UFO investigations usually rely on several independent lines of evidence.

Examples include:

* multiple eyewitnesses interviewed separately;

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* original photographs or video files;
* precise timestamps;
* weather records;
* astronomical comparisons;
* aircraft and maritime traffic records;
* radar returns;
* independent technical image analysis;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://mufon.com/2026/02/13/a-mufon-photo-analysis-is-this-green-orb-communicating-a-forensic-image-analysis-of-a-viral-uap-claim-by-seth-h-feinstein-mufon-photo-analyst/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[mufon.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

The Cape Verde reports rarely contain more than one or two of these elements.

Instead, many rely almost entirely on a single witness account or a single photograph. Because there is no independent verification, investigators cannot accurately reconstruct what occurred or eliminate ordinary possibilities.

This absence of corroboration is the principal reason the evidential value remains low.

## How Reliable Are the Available Sources?

The quality of evidence is also affected by where the reports originate.

Most accessible Cape Verde [sightings]({{ 'sightings-4905ad/' | relative_url }}) come from UFO reporting websites, witness submissions or media reproductions rather than official investigations. Such sources can preserve valuable witness testimony, but they generally do not perform the detailed forensic work needed to determine what an object actually was.

Similarly, retrospective reports submitted many years after the alleged event are inherently harder to verify. Memories fade, supporting records disappear and opportunities to compare the observation with aircraft movements, satellite passes or meteorological conditions are largely lost.

That does not mean every report is inaccurate. It means confidence in any specific interpretation—whether ordinary or extraordinary—is necessarily reduced.

## The Most Defensible Conclusion

Taken together, the available material supports a cautious assessment.

Cape Verde has a small number of genuine reports of unusual aerial observations. Those reports are sufficient to establish that witnesses believed they saw something unexpected, and that photographs or videos exist for some incidents.

However, the evidence does not establish unusual performance, unknown technology or extraterrestrial origin. Most claims depend on incomplete documentation, compressed imagery and isolated witness accounts that cannot be independently verified. Conventional explanations cannot always be confirmed, but neither can they be confidently excluded.

For that reason, the available evidence is best described as **weak to moderate for the existence of unexplained observations, but weak for any extraordinary interpretation**. The cases remain unresolved primarily because the surviving evidence is incomplete, not because it demonstrates phenomena beyond current scientific understanding.

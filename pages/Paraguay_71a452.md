## Introduction

Three episodes dominate the national record: mid-century reports passed into United States military archives, a widely discussed aerial encounter on 8 June 1991, and the continuing stream of photographs and videos circulated through Paraguayan news and social media. Their evidential value differs sharply. The 1991 incident has identifiable pilots, air-traffic personnel and contemporary reports; many modern cases have only compressed video, uncertain dates or no original file. Paraguay’s UFO history is therefore best understood as a fragmented aviation and cultural archive rather than a catalogue of confirmed extraordinary craft.

## What Paraguay’s UFO record actually contains

There is no known Paraguayan equivalent of the large, searchable government UFO archives maintained by countries such as Brazil or the United States. Public discussion instead depends heavily on privately collected newspaper material, interviews and documents said to have circulated through aviation or defence institutions.

The most extensive published national compilation is associated with Paraguayan researcher Ronald Maidana Torres. ## A chronology of the strongest-known cases

### Mid-century reports: Asunción, Areguá and the Chaco

Reports from the late 1940s and early 1950s form the earliest modern cluster regularly linked to official records. Paraguayan coverage of recently publicised American files has described oval or luminous objects seen from Asunción, Areguá and the Chaco. Some accounts also refer to an alleged landing or low-altitude object near school grounds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.extra.com.py/mundo/paraguay-esta-entre-los-informes-sobre-ovnis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: extra.com.py">[Extra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">extra.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Extra Paraguay está entre los informes sobre OVNIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Paraguay está entre los informes sobre OVNIS - Diario ExtraMay 15, 2026 — Paraguay figura en documentos recientemente desclasificado...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2026</span></span></span>

These cases are historically interesting because they emerged during the first international “flying saucer” wave, when military and diplomatic channels were actively collecting reports from many countries. That context also complicates interpretation. Press excitement, limited aviation knowledge, Cold War secrecy and poor astronomical identification all contributed to the volume of reports received by early UFO investigations.

A later independent catalogue of Project Blue Book “unknowns” lists a February 1963 report from “Ascension, Paraguay”, in which a student allegedly saw an object descend near a school while other residents reported a fast-moving object overhead. The entry is brief and relies partly on secondary catalogues. The inconsistent place-name rendering and absence of a complete evidential file make it unsuitable as proof of a landing, although it may preserve the outline of a real local report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/download/BernardSieglerTechnicsAndTime1TheFaultOfEpimetheus/Brad%20Sparks%20-%20Comprehensive%20Catalog%20of%201%2C600%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20UFO%20Unknowns.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveComprehensive Catalog of 1600 Project Blue Book UFO...October 15, 2009 — 2 Oct 2009 — Comprehensive Catalog of 1,600 Pro...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 15, 2009</span></span></span>

The principal weakness across this period is not necessarily witness honesty but missing data. Precise observation times, viewing directions, angular size, duration, weather, aircraft movements and original negatives are usually unavailable. Without them, investigators cannot reliably test planets, meteors, balloons, aircraft or unusual atmospheric effects.

### The 17 September 1985 Asunción episode

A more vivid case entered Paraguayan popular memory in September 1985, when journalists and other witnesses followed or watched an unusual aerial object around Asunción. A later recollection described how the episode became a newsroom pursuit and was subsequently revived in television documentaries. One additional witness reportedly said he had observed the phenomenon while travelling on a commercial aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lanacion.com.py/gran-diario-domingo/2023/10/01/la-aventura-de-perseguir-un-ovni-aunque-la-nasa-no-sepa-de-que-se-trata/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lanacion.com.py">[La Nación]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lanacion.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La NaciónLa aventura de perseguir un ovni, aunque la NASA no...October 1, 2023 — 1 Oct 2023 — Sin embargo, aquel ovni del 17 de setiembr...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 1, 2023</span></span></span>

The case has social value because it shows how a shared sighting becomes part of urban memory. Multiple people remembered an unusual object, and the story was not created solely by an anonymous internet post. Nevertheless, retrospective testimony given decades later is vulnerable to memory convergence: witnesses can retain the central event while gradually aligning details through repeated discussion, journalism and documentaries.

No publicly accessible package of radar records, meteorological observations, original photographs and contemporaneous statements has been identified. The incident therefore remains unresolved in the ordinary sense—its cause is not securely known—but evidentially incomplete.

### The 8 June 1991 aviation encounter

The most important Paraguayan case is the reported encounter of 8 June 1991. Paraguayan coverage states that an unknown object appeared amid active air traffic and accompanied or approached both a Cessna and an aircraft operated by the national airline. The event reportedly generated aviation reports and was recorded in material held by the Ministry of Defence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/abc-revista/el-retorno-de-la-fantasia-1503677.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[ABC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABCEl retorno de la fantasía</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABCEl retorno de la fantasía</span></span></span>

Later international UFO literature referred to the episode as an aircraft interception over Paraguay, drawing on an article published in the *MUFON UFO Journal* in 1994. MUFON is a civilian organisation that receives and investigates UFO reports; its publications can preserve useful testimony, but they are not equivalent to independent aviation-safety findings or peer-reviewed scientific analysis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dokumen.pub/unidentified-flying-objects-briefing-document-the-best-available-evidence-0307492273-9780307492272.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dokumen.pub">[dokumen.pub]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dokumen.pub</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on dokumen.pub.</span></span></span>

This incident ranks above most Paraguayan cases because it appears to involve trained observers, multiple aircraft and air-traffic personnel. Those features reduce the likelihood of a purely personal hallucination or fabricated social-media post. They do not eliminate ordinary explanations. Pilots can misjudge distance and speed at night, especially when an object lacks a visible background. Bright planets, other aircraft viewed at unusual angles, reflections, atmospheric propagation and radar anomalies can all create convincing impressions of extraordinary motion.

The decisive material would be the original flight reports, radar plots, radio transcripts, weather records and full statements from each observer taken before they discussed the event with one another. Public summaries indicate that documentation once existed, but the complete file is not readily available for independent examination. The responsible classification is therefore **a comparatively strong witness case with insufficient public data**, not a confirmed encounter with an exotic craft.

### Tañarandy and local memory

Tañarandy, in the southern department of Misiones, has developed its own local UFO story. ### Photographs from Paraguarí

In 2007, a public employee in Paraguarí presented a photograph containing a small luminous or spherical feature that he noticed only after downloading and enlarging the image. He said he had been photographing a statue in cloudy or rainy conditions and had not consciously observed the object while taking the picture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.com/en-paraguari-muestran-la-foto-supuesto-ovni-n23518" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.com">[Última Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Última Hora En Paraguarí muestran la foto de supuesto ovni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Última Hora En Paraguarí muestran la foto de supuesto ovni</span></span></span>

Such photographs are intrinsically weak evidence. An object discovered only after enlargement may be an insect, bird, raindrop, lens reflection, compression artefact or distant debris. A single image generally cannot establish distance, size or speed. Without the original file, full sequence, camera settings and comparison frames, the photograph shows an unidentified image feature rather than an independently observed flying object.

## Why reports vary by region

Paraguay’s reported cases are not evenly distributed in either character or documentation.

**Asunción and the central region** produce the most publicly visible reports because they contain the largest concentration of population, journalists, airports and cameras. These cases are more likely to involve multiple witnesses, but they also occur in crowded airspace filled with aircraft, helicopters, drones and artificial lights.

**The eastern and southern departments**, including Paraguarí and Misiones, feature more localised stories tied to particular towns, hills or community traditions. Reports from these areas often survive through oral testimony, photographs or regional journalism rather than formal aviation records.

**The Chaco** has a different appeal. Its large, sparsely populated landscape and dark skies make unusual lights more noticeable, while long distances make identification difficult. A bright object near the horizon may appear to move with a vehicle because of the parallax effect: when the observer moves, a distant light seems to keep pace. Sparse reference points also make altitude and speed exceptionally hard to judge.

Geography alone does not demonstrate that any region experiences more genuinely anomalous activity. Report numbers are shaped by population, media access, visibility, military and commercial flight paths, and the willingness of witnesses to speak publicly.

## Official records and the transparency gap

The central documentary problem is that Paraguay has no widely advertised, searchable national UFO archive. ## The evidence ledger for Paraguay's UFO record
### What is confirmed

It is confirmed that Paraguay has produced recurring reports of unusual aerial phenomena, including reports by pilots and air-traffic personnel. It is also confirmed that some Paraguayan cases entered foreign military or private UFO archives, and that national researchers have assembled a substantial historical collection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/abc-revista/el-retorno-de-la-fantasia-1503677.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.com.py">[com.py]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.com.py</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABCEl retorno de la fantasía</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABCEl retorno de la fantasía</span></span></span>

These facts justify historical and aviation-oriented research. They do not confirm alien visitation.

### What remains contested

The 1991 aviation encounter remains the most credible unresolved claim because it reportedly involved several professional observers and formal reports. Its classification remains contested because the full primary record is not publicly available.

The mid-century school and Chaco reports are also unresolved at the level of historical narrative. Their evidential position is weaker because available summaries are short, sometimes inconsistent and often separated from the original documentation.

The 1985 Asunción episode is supported by remembered testimony but lacks a publicly accessible technical file. It is better regarded as a collective unexplained observation than as proof of a structured craft.

## The enduring value of Paraguay’s UFO history

Paraguay’s UFO record is valuable less for proving extraordinary visitation than for showing how unexplained observations move between aviation systems, newspapers, private archives and local folklore. Its strongest incident—the June 1991 aerial encounter—deserves preservation and renewed documentary review because it appears to have generated formal contemporary records. The earlier cases deserve archival work that distinguishes original documents from repeated summaries.

At present, the national evidence supports four conclusions. Unusual aerial observations have occurred; some involved credible and professionally trained witnesses; official or semi-official records have existed; and no publicly available Paraguayan case provides independently verified evidence of extraterrestrial technology.

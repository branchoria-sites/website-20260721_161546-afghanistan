## Introduction

In November 2024, many people across the Eastern Caribbean, including observers in [Saint Lucia]({{ 'what-really-flew-over-saint-lucia/' | relative_url }}), reported an extraordinary glowing object crossing the evening sky. To witnesses, it appeared unlike a conventional aircraft or meteor: a brilliant white head was followed by a broad, luminous plume that seemed to expand into a jellyfish- or comet-like shape. Social media quickly filled with speculation about an unidentified flying object.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Saint_Lucia_85ac35_starship_2024_event_8462fb-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Starship Event illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Saint_Lucia_85ac35_starship_2024_event_8462fb-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Saint_Lucia_85ac35_starship_2024_event_8462fb-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

Subsequent analysis showed that this was not an unexplained aerial phenomenon but the highly visible exhaust plume from SpaceX's sixth Starship test flight. The timing, flight path and appearance all matched the launch from Starbase, Texas, on 19 November 2024. Rather than becoming Saint Lucia's strongest UFO case, the event has become one of the clearest modern examples of how unusual spaceflight can be mistaken for something mysterious when viewed from hundreds or even thousands of kilometres away.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Sixth Flight TestNovember 19, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2024</span></span></span>

## How the Starship timing matched

The identification became convincing because several independent lines of evidence pointed to the same explanation.

SpaceX launched Starship Flight 6 from Starbase in South Texas on 19 November 2024 at approximately 22:00 UTC. Mission tracking, telemetry and official launch records place the vehicle on a trajectory over the Gulf of Mexico before continuing on its planned suborbital path toward splashdown in the Indian Ocean.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[spacex.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Sixth Flight TestNovember 19, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2024</span></span></span>

The reported Caribbean [sightings]({{ 'sightings-99ab30/' | relative_url }}) occurred during the same time window that the vehicle was climbing into space.

The match involved more than simple coincidence:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Time:** Reports coincided with the known launch schedule.
* **Direction:** The object appeared where the rocket would have been visible from the Eastern Caribbean.
* **Appearance:** Witness descriptions matched the expanding exhaust cloud expected from a high-altitude rocket.
* **Duration:** The several-minute visibility matched the ascent phase rather than a brief astronomical event.

</div>

Unlike many UFO reports, this event therefore had an independently documented aerospace event occurring at exactly the right time.

## Why the object looked so unusual

The appearance that led many observers to think they were seeing an unknown craft is well understood in atmospheric physics.

### Sunlight illuminated the exhaust far above Earth

Although observers in Saint Lucia were already in twilight or darkness, Starship had climbed to altitudes where its exhaust remained illuminated by the Sun.

This created a bright white cloud suspended against a darkening sky, making the plume appear almost self-luminous rather than simply reflecting daylight.

### The plume expanded rapidly

At high altitude the atmosphere is extremely thin.

Rocket exhaust therefore expands into a huge cloud instead of remaining as the narrow trail familiar from aircraft. The resulting shape often resembles:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a jellyfish,<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w6hIXB62bUE" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[youtube.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Sightings Explained: Space Jellyfish</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO or Rocket Plume? * a glowing teardrop,
* a giant comet, or
* an umbrella spreading across the sky.

</div>

This appearance has accompanied many rocket launches worldwide and is one of the most common sources of dramatic but ultimately identified UFO reports.

### Perspective exaggerated the effect

Observers were viewing the rocket from a very large distance.

The vehicle itself was far too small to see directly. Instead, the visible object was primarily the illuminated exhaust cloud, which can span many kilometres while appearing compact from hundreds or thousands of kilometres away.

Because there are few familiar visual cues at such distances, people naturally struggle to judge its altitude, speed and size.

## Why the event matters for Saint Lucia's UFO record

Within Saint Lucia's broader catalogue of reported unidentified aerial phenomena, the November 2024 event is significant precisely because it demonstrates successful identification rather than enduring mystery.

Earlier reports from the island often consist of single witnesses, limited documentation or photographs that cannot be independently verified. By contrast, the Starship sighting was:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* witnessed across a wide geographical area;
* tied to a precisely timed aerospace event;
* explainable using well-understood atmospheric effects; and
* consistent with observations made simultaneously from numerous Caribbean locations.

</div>

As a result, it represents one of the strongest evidence-based case studies available for understanding how spectacular but entirely human-made phenomena can generate genuine UFO reports.

## What the incident teaches about future sightings

The Starship event illustrates an increasingly important challenge as commercial spaceflight becomes more frequent.

Large reusable rockets produce visual effects that many people have never previously encountered. When launches occur near sunrise or sunset, illuminated exhaust plumes can appear extraordinarily bright and can be visible across vast regions, sometimes hundreds or thousands of kilometres from the launch site.

For investigators examining future reports from Saint Lucia, the November 2024 case provides a practical checklist:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--checklist" markdown="1">

* check whether a rocket launch occurred at the reported time;
* compare the reported direction with the launch trajectory;
* consider whether the Sun could still illuminate a high-altitude plume;
* examine reports from neighbouring Caribbean islands; and
* only after excluding known aerospace activity consider less conventional explanations.

</div>

Far from weakening UFO research, this approach strengthens it by separating well-understood atmospheric and aerospace phenomena from cases that genuinely remain unidentified. The November 2024 Starship sighting therefore stands as Saint Lucia's best-documented modern UFO report—and also its clearest demonstrated misidentification.

      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

## Introduction

The reported UFO encounters over Golubovci in early 1975 are the best-known military UFO case associated with present-day [Montenegro]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-montenegros-ufo/' | relative_url }}). According to later testimony from Yugoslav Air Force officers, pilots of the 172nd Aviation Regiment repeatedly encountered a luminous object during night training flights over southern Montenegro and the nearby Adriatic. Some accounts describe attempted interceptions, a proposed deployment of MiG-21 fighters, and internal military reporting. Yet despite the story's prominence in regional UFO literature, almost all publicly available evidence comes from memoirs, interviews and regimental histories published decades after the events rather than from contemporaneous operational records. That combination makes the case historically intriguing but evidentially difficult to verify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

## Night flights over Golubovci and the Adriatic

The reported encounters took place while the 172nd Aviation Regiment was based at Golubovci Air Base outside Titograd (now Podgorica). According to retired General Zvonko Jurjević, who was then serving as acting commander of the regiment, the phenomenon first appeared shortly after the New Year in January 1975 during routine night training missions flown mainly in Yugoslav-built G-2 Galeb and J-21 Jastreb aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://conexaoufo.com/en/former-yugoslav-air-force-commander-reports-ufo-encounter-and-pursuit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: conexaoufo.com">[UFO Connection]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">conexaoufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ConnectionFormer Yugoslav Air Force Commander Reports UFO Encounter and Pursuit - Conexão UFO...</span></span></span>

Jurjević consistently described the object as an intensely bright sphere that repeatedly changed colour between white, yellow, orange and red. He claimed that it appeared unexpectedly, remained visible for several minutes and seemed to draw the pilots' attention deliberately. One unusual detail repeated across several interviews is that the object allegedly appeared at roughly the same point in each training sortie—around fifty minutes after take-off—which led crews to begin anticipating its arrival.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://conexaoufo.com/en/former-yugoslav-air-force-commander-reports-ufo-encounter-and-pursuit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: conexaoufo.com">[conexaoufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">conexaoufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ConnectionFormer Yugoslav Air Force Commander Reports UFO Encounter and Pursuit - Conexão UFO...</span></span></span>

The reported observations were not described as a single isolated encounter. Instead, witnesses later portrayed them as a series of incidents extending over several weeks. According to the regimental narrative, multiple crews observed the object independently during separate flights, reducing the likelihood that the reports stemmed from one pilot's misidentification or fatigue. Some later versions claim that as many as twelve aircraft carrying twenty-four pilots witnessed the phenomenon simultaneously during one exercise, although these numerical details originate from retrospective accounts rather than surviving operational documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

The geographical setting also matters. Southern Montenegro and the Adriatic coastline provide wide night horizons where bright celestial bodies, atmospheric effects and distant lights can sometimes create deceptive visual impressions. However, the pilots involved argued that they were accustomed to night flying in the region and regarded the observed behaviour as inconsistent with familiar astronomical or aviation phenomena. That assessment reflects witness opinion rather than an independently verified conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://conexaoufo.com/en/former-yugoslav-air-force-commander-reports-ufo-encounter-and-pursuit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: conexaoufo.com">[UFO Connection]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">conexaoufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ConnectionFormer Yugoslav Air Force Commander Reports UFO Encounter and Pursuit - Conexão UFO...</span></span></span>

## The alleged MiG-21 interception plan

As the reported sightings continued, later testimony claims that the regiment sought to determine whether faster aircraft could identify or intercept the object.

According to Jurjević, a plan was devised to bring MiG-21 fighters into the operation without informing the regular training formation. The intention, as later described, was to allow the mysterious object to focus on the slower training aircraft while concealed interceptors attempted to approach at high speed. The story has become one of the defining features of the Golubovci case because it suggests that the military leadership considered the reports operationally significant enough to justify special planning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://conexaoufo.com/en/former-yugoslav-air-force-commander-reports-ufo-encounter-and-pursuit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: conexaoufo.com">[conexaoufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">conexaoufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ConnectionFormer Yugoslav Air Force Commander Reports UFO Encounter and Pursuit - Conexão UFO...</span></span></span>

Several later articles also claim that experienced pilots, including Ivan Mamić and other officers, participated in interception attempts using MiG-21 aircraft. Some retellings state that the fighters briefly approached or obtained radar lock before the object accelerated away, while others simply report unsuccessful pursuit. These details are not entirely consistent across sources, illustrating how the narrative has evolved over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hrsvijet.net/index.php/arhiva-clanaka-hrsvijet-net/137-arhiva-stari-hrsvijet-net-1/20808-piloti-jna-jurili-nlo-mig-ovima-1975-pokuavali-presresti-nepoznate-letjelice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hrsvijet.net">[hrsvijet.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hrsvijet.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">H Rsvijet.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Piloti JNA jurili NLO: MIG-ovima 1975. pokušavali presresti nepoznate letjelice...</span></span></span>

Another frequently repeated claim is that the wider effort eventually involved thirteen aircraft and twenty-six pilots. While this figure appears in regional reporting, no publicly released military record has been found confirming the exact number of aircraft or personnel involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufo.com.br/forca-aerea-da-iugoslavia-perseguiu-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufo.com.br">[UFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufo.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOForça Aérea da Iugoslávia perseguiu UFOs – Revista UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOForça Aérea da Iugoslávia perseguiu UFOs – Revista UFO</span></span></span>

## Which claims can and cannot be verified

The historical value of the Golubovci encounters depends heavily on distinguishing documented facts from later recollections.

Reasonably well supported are several basic points:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The 172nd Aviation Regiment operated from Golubovci during the relevant period.
* Zvonko Jurjević later served as a senior Yugoslav Air Force officer and publicly associated himself with the incidents.
* The case entered the public record through a regimental history, later interviews and repeated regional media coverage rather than anonymous internet stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[dni.org.ro]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

</div>

Much less secure are the more dramatic operational claims. Publicly accessible sources have not produced authenticated flight logs, radar recordings, cockpit recordings, meteorological analyses, intelligence files or the reported military commission's final assessment. Without these primary materials, it is impossible to independently reconstruct flight paths, speeds, altitudes or the precise sequence of encounters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

Radar reporting is particularly inconsistent. Some versions state that the object was invisible to military radar, while others describe successful radar tracking or calculations of extraordinary speeds. Because these claims differ across later retellings and no original radar data have been released, they cannot presently be reconciled with confidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

Likewise, assertions that the object anticipated secret interception plans, deliberately manoeuvred around aircraft or demonstrated extreme acceleration remain witness interpretations rather than independently verified observations. Those elements depend entirely upon retrospective testimony recorded decades after the events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://conexaoufo.com/en/former-yugoslav-air-force-commander-reports-ufo-encounter-and-pursuit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: conexaoufo.com">[UFO Connection]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">conexaoufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ConnectionFormer Yugoslav Air Force Commander Reports UFO Encounter and Pursuit - Conexão UFO...</span></span></span>

## Why the case remains important despite limited evidence

The Golubovci encounters continue to receive attention because they differ from many civilian UFO reports in several respects. The principal witnesses were trained military aviators with experience conducting night operations, the sightings were described as recurring rather than singular, and multiple named personnel later endorsed broadly similar accounts. These features make the case noteworthy within the history of reported UFO incidents in Montenegro and the former Yugoslavia.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

At the same time, the case illustrates the limits of historical reconstruction when primary documentation is unavailable. Nearly every significant claim that gives the story its reputation—the interception attempts, radar performance, extraordinary manoeuvres and internal military investigation—rests on recollections published decades after 1975. The absence of contemporaneous operational evidence means that the encounters cannot presently be confirmed, but neither can they be conclusively explained or dismissed.

Within Montenegro's broader catalogue of UFO reports, the 1975 Golubovci incidents therefore occupy a distinctive position: they are neither a confirmed encounter with an extraordinary craft nor a case conclusively resolved through conventional evidence. Instead, they remain a historically significant military witness case whose enduring reputation derives as much from the credibility of its participants as from the continuing absence of the records needed to test their claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/aviatia-militara-iugoslava-si-ozn-urile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[dni.org.ro]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 31, 2018</span></span></span>

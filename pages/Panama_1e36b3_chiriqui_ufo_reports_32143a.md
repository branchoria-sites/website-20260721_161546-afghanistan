## Introduction

Chiriquí occupies a distinctive place in [Panama]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-panamas-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO folklore because it combines some of the country's best natural observing conditions with several of its most frequently repeated photographic claims. The province's high elevations, relatively dark skies, broad horizons and active amateur astronomy community mean that more people spend time looking upwards than in many other parts of Panama. Those same conditions also increase opportunities to mistake ordinary astronomical or aviation phenomena for something extraordinary.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Panama_1e36b3_chiriqui_ufo_reports_32143a-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Chiriqui illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Panama_1e36b3_chiriqui_ufo_reports_32143a-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Panama_1e36b3_chiriqui_ufo_reports_32143a-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The available evidence does not show that Chiriquí has produced stronger UFO evidence than the rest of Panama. Instead, it has generated a mixture of widely shared photographs, eyewitness reports and scientific sky observation. The most discussed modern case remains the blurred Bugaba mobile-phone photographs from 2016, while Boquete has become known internationally for its Optical SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) observatory rather than for confirmed UFO sightings. Together, these illustrate why Chiriquí is an important case study in distinguishing unusual observations from well-documented evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.critica.com.pa/chiriqui/ovni-la-vista-241001" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: critica.com.pa">[Crítica]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">critica.com.pa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crítica¡Ovni a la vista! &#124; Critica</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">April 15, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 15, 2016</span></span></span>

These factors increase the number of genuine observations but not necessarily the number of unexplained phenomena. Bright planets near the horizon, Starlink satellite trains, high-altitude aircraft reflecting sunlight after sunset, weather balloons and camera artefacts all become easier to notice under exceptionally clear conditions. For investigators, favourable observing conditions reduce some sources of error while simultaneously making distant conventional objects more conspicuous.

This distinction is important because many reports originate from visual impressions without supporting measurements, multiple observation points or original image files that would allow reliable reconstruction.

## The 2016 Bugaba mobile-phone photographs

The best-known Chiriquí photographic case occurred on 6 April 2016 in La Concepción, Bugaba.

According to the contemporary newspaper report, local resident José Manuel Aizpurua photographed an object at approximately 5:57 pm using a mobile phone. Three photographs were reportedly taken within about one minute. The witness described a clear sky, stated that no vultures were present, and recalled seeing small blue points associated with the object. The published images showed only a blurred silhouette because they were captured while the phone was moving.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.critica.com.pa/chiriqui/ovni-la-vista-241001" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: critica.com.pa">[Crítica]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">critica.com.pa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Crítica¡Ovni a la vista! &#124; Critica</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">April 15, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 15, 2016</span></span></span>

Although the photographs circulated widely within Panamanian UFO discussions, several limitations prevent them from serving as strong evidence:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* The published images are low-resolution and visibly blurred.
* No original image files with full metadata have been publicly analysed.
* There is no confirmed triangulation from additional photographers.
* No radar, astronomical or aviation data accompanied the report.
* The object's distance, size and speed cannot be calculated from the available material.

</div>

These missing elements are more significant than the appearance of the object itself. A small nearby bird, insect, balloon or wind-blown object photographed while a camera is moving can produce indistinct shapes that resemble structured craft after enlargement. Without geometric information, investigators cannot distinguish between a nearby small object and a distant large one.

For that reason, the Bugaba photographs are best classified as **unresolved because of insufficient evidence**, not as confirmation of an extraordinary craft.

## Boquete's highland skies and Optical SETI

Boquete's international reputation comes less from UFO sightings than from scientific observation of the sky.

Since 2010, the privately operated Boquete Optical SETI Observatory has participated in research searching for brief laser pulses that could indicate technologically produced optical signals from other star systems. The observatory has developed specialised photon-detection equipment and has observed thousands of stellar targets using methods designed to minimise false detections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.01956" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

The observatory gained wider scientific attention when it participated in observations of the unusual star KIC 8462852 (Boyajian's Star), whose irregular dimming briefly prompted speculation about artificial megastructures. Boquete's optical observations found no detectable laser pulses consistent with an artificial beacon, and the published work concluded without evidence for extraterrestrial signalling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://technosearch.seti.org/wp-content/uploads/2018-09/Schuetz_2016_ApJL_825_L5.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: technosearch.seti.org">[Technosearch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">technosearch.seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Schuetz 2016 ApJL 825 L5</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OPTICAL SETI OBSERVATIONS OF THE ANOMALOUS STAR KIC 8462852June 23, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2016</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because the observatory demonstrates an evidence-based approach to unusual claims:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* observations use calibrated instruments rather than casual photography;
* candidate detections require repeatability and statistical testing;
* apparent anomalies are examined against instrumental and natural explanations before extraordinary conclusions are considered.

</div>

Rather than supporting local UFO folklore, Boquete's scientific programme illustrates how rigorous astronomical observation differs from anecdotal skywatching.

## What should be tested before calling a sighting unexplained?

Chiriquí's geography makes several conventional explanations especially important to examine.

**Astronomical objects.** Venus, Jupiter and bright stars near the horizon often appear unusually bright in clear mountain air. Atmospheric turbulence can make them shimmer, change colour or appear to move slowly relative to foreground features.

**Satellites.** Bright satellites and Starlink constellations have become common sources of reports across Panama. Under dark skies they remain visible far longer than observers expect.

**Aircraft.** Aircraft approaching or departing regional airports can appear nearly stationary when flying towards an observer. Sunlight reflected from fuselages or contrails after local sunset can create striking luminous effects.

**Balloons and airborne debris.** Wind-driven balloons, lightweight plastic and other airborne objects can drift slowly, rotate and produce irregular reflections that resemble structured craft in photographs.

**Camera limitations.** Mobile-phone digital zoom, autofocus errors, rolling shutter effects, compression artefacts and motion blur frequently transform small distant objects into apparently unusual shapes. The Bugaba photographs demonstrate how image quality alone can prevent meaningful identification.

Because these mechanisms are well documented, they should be investigated before concluding that a genuinely unidentified object has been observed.

## How Chiriquí fits within Panama's wider UFO record

Compared with other Panamanian regions, Chiriquí stands out because it combines favourable observing conditions with a small number of repeatedly cited photographic cases. However, it has not produced publicly available evidence such as synchronised multi-camera recordings, recovered physical material, verified radar tracking or independently confirmed scientific measurements that would elevate its reports above the broader national record.

Instead, Chiriquí illustrates a recurring pattern seen throughout Panama:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* visually striking photographs attract attention even when they contain limited analytical value;
* excellent observing conditions increase both genuine skywatching and ordinary misidentifications;
* scientific astronomical work in the region demonstrates how extraordinary claims require far stronger documentation than eyewitness testimony or blurred images alone.

</div>

For readers examining Panama's regional UFO history, Chiriquí is therefore significant not because it offers the country's strongest evidence for unidentified craft, but because it clearly shows the contrast between compelling visual reports and the level of documentation required for those reports to withstand critical investigation.

    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

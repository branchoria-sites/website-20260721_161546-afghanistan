## Introduction

[Argentina]({{ 'which-argentina-ufo-cases-still-resist/' | relative_url }})'s Air Force has become one of the more transparent official bodies examining civilian UFO reports, but its published findings are far less mysterious than many popular accounts suggest. Since establishing the Commission for the Study of Aerospace Phenomena (CEFAE) in 2011 and reorganising it as the Aerospace Identification Centre (CIAE) in 2019, the Air Force has operated a public reporting system, issued annual case reviews, and explained in detail how seemingly unusual sightings are investigated. Rather than treating every report as evidence of an unknown craft, the centre attempts to identify ordinary aerospace, astronomical or photographic causes using witness testimony, digital evidence and technical analysis. Official reports consistently show that the overwhelming majority of accepted cases can be traced to conventional explanations rather than genuinely unexplained phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.argentina.gob.ar/fuerzaaerea/centro-de-identificacion-aeroespacial" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: argentina.gob.ar">[Argentina]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span></span></span>

## How civilian reports enter the Air Force system

Argentina's Air Force does not investigate every claim that appears on social media or in the press. Instead, the CIAE asks members of the public to submit reports together with supporting material such as photographs, video, dates, locations and witness information. Cases lacking sufficient evidence are generally not accepted for formal analysis because investigators cannot reconstruct the event reliably. This approach is intended to improve evidential quality rather than maximise the number of investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.argentina.gob.ar/fuerzaaerea/centro-de-identificacion-aeroespacial" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: argentina.gob.ar">[Argentina]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span></span></span>

The centre also makes clear that serving the public by explaining sightings is only one of its functions. Its primary institutional role is to analyse aerospace events of interest to the Argentine Air Force, while civilian UFO reports provide an opportunity both to inform the public and to refine investigative methods.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.argentina.gob.ar/fuerzaaerea/centro-de-identificacion-aeroespacial" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: argentina.gob.ar">[Argentina]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial &#124; Argentina.gob.ar</span></span></span>

## What Type A and Type B actually mean

A frequent misunderstanding is that any case not placed into a fully explained category remains mysterious. Argentina instead follows a simplified classification derived from the methodology used by France's GEIPAN, the long-running official aerospace phenomena investigation programme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://disclosdex.com/organizations/argentine-air-force-ciae" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: disclosdex.com">[Disclosdex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">disclosdex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Argentine Air Force Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) &#124; Disclosdex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Argentine Air Force Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) &#124; DisclosdexApril 4, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 4, 2019</span></span></span>

The categories most commonly used in CIAE annual reports are:

* **Type A** – the object has been identified with confidence through direct evidence. The explanation matches the available observations.
* **Type B** – investigators conclude that the explanation is strongly supported by the available evidence, even if every detail cannot be demonstrated with absolute certainty.

Importantly, neither category represents an unexplained aerial phenomenon. Both indicate that investigators believe the observation has an identifiable conventional origin, differing mainly in the level of certainty supported by the available evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparency...</span></span></span>

The reports also note that conclusions are not considered immune from revision. If genuinely new evidence is produced, the CIAE states that a case can be reopened rather than remaining permanently closed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2024-informe-ciae-2024" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2024 informe ciae 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2024, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2025 · UFO Transparency...</span></span></span>

## Why modern reports are easier to solve

The contrast between historical Argentine UFO cases and recent investigations largely reflects improvements in available evidence.

Older incidents often relied upon:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* witness memories recorded days or weeks later;
* incomplete photographs;
* newspaper summaries;
* missing radar or flight data.

</div>

Modern civilian submissions increasingly contain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* original digital image files;
* embedded metadata;
* complete video sequences;
* accurate timestamps;
* precise geographic locations;
* publicly available satellite and aircraft tracking information.

</div>

These additional data sources allow investigators to compare reported sightings with known aircraft routes, satellite passes, astronomical objects and atmospheric conditions far more effectively than was possible decades ago.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2024-informe-ciae-2024" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2024 informe ciae 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2024, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2025 · UFO Transparency...</span></span></span>

## Aircraft, satellites, drones and camera artefacts

The most striking feature of Argentina's annual reports is not a catalogue of unexplained objects but the remarkable consistency of ordinary explanations.

Across recent reporting years, investigators repeatedly identify:

* commercial aircraft observed under unusual lighting;
* satellites, particularly highly reflective satellite constellations;

* bright planets and stars viewed under unusual atmospheric conditions;

* raindrops or dirt on glass;
* digital image artefacts and edited photographs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparency...</span></span></span>

### Satellites and orbital objects

The rapid expansion of satellite constellations has introduced bright moving lights that many observers have never previously encountered.

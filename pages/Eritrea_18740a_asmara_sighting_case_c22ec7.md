## Introduction

The Asmara UFO mystery is [Eritrea]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-eritreas-ufo/' | relative_url }})’s most documented historical unidentified aerial report, but its importance comes from the surviving evidence rather than from proof of an extraordinary explanation. The case concerns reports made near Asmara in the late 1940s–1950 period, when Eritrea was under British administration, in which witnesses described unusual objects in the sky and officials exchanged correspondence while attempting to identify them. The available records show a genuine investigation of an unexplained observation, but they do not establish that the objects were spacecraft or any non-human technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

The case remains interesting because it contains more than a single eyewitness story: it includes official correspondence, competing interpretations, and attempts by military and meteorological authorities to test ordinary explanations. At the same time, the evidence has clear limitations. There are no known photographs, radar records, physical materials, or modern sensor measurements linked to the event. The Asmara report is therefore best understood as an unresolved historical aerial observation rather than confirmed evidence of a UFO in the extraterrestrial sense.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

## Witness reports and official correspondence

### What the Asmara witnesses actually reported

The surviving records describe several observers in and around Asmara reporting unusual objects, including descriptions of “spheres” and “discs” in the sky. One important witness, F. E. Stafford of the British Administration in Eritrea, reported seeing objects and questioned whether military activity near Asmara could explain them. According to the archived correspondence, Stafford noted that he had been told the objects were of the same type and sought clarification about possible military balloon use in the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

The reports focused on two apparently different behaviours:

* A slower-moving object that some officials considered possibly consistent with a balloon.
* A rapidly moving and apparently erratic object that proved harder for investigators to explain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because the case was not treated as a single mysterious object with one agreed description. Different witnesses and officials were attempting to reconcile observations that may have involved separate phenomena. That uncertainty became central to the later debate over explanations.

### Why the official record is significant

The strongest evidence for the Asmara case is not the dramatic nature of the sighting but the existence of administrative records showing that officials seriously considered possible causes. The British authorities corresponded with meteorological and military bodies rather than simply dismissing the reports. The National Archives’ material on UFO investigations shows that many historical reports were handled through similar processes: witness accounts were collected, [possible explanations]({{ 'possible-explanations-ffb2c0/' | relative_url }}) were proposed, and unresolved cases remained classified as unidentified rather than automatically treated as extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

One official assessment quoted in the archived material argued that one object was probably balloon-borne, while another rapidly moving object remained unexplained. A British Administration official later stated that the meteorological explanation did not fully account for the rapidly moving object seen by multiple people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

The correspondence therefore provides evidence of an unresolved investigation, not evidence of a confirmed unknown technology.

## Possible explanations considered

The Asmara case attracted attention because ordinary explanations appeared to fit some parts of the report but not others. The main explanations considered were balloons, astronomical objects, and aircraft-related activity.

### Weather balloons and atmospheric equipment

A balloon explanation was one of the earliest possibilities considered. Balloons were commonly used during the mid-20th century for meteorological observations and other scientific purposes, and their movement could appear unusual to observers because winds often differ at different altitudes. A balloon may drift, change apparent direction, or seem to move irregularly when viewed from the ground.

In the Asmara records, officials suggested that at least one reported object could have been balloon-related. However, some administrators argued that the reported speed and movement of another object did not fit a simple balloon explanation. The disagreement reflects a common challenge in historical UFO investigations: a plausible explanation for one observation may not explain every detail reported by witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

### Astronomical objects and perception effects

Astronomical explanations were also considered. Bright planets or stars can sometimes appear to move, flicker, or change position because of atmospheric distortion, especially when viewed near the horizon or through unstable air.

However, officials involved in the Asmara correspondence questioned whether astronomical objects could explain reports of apparent rapid and irregular movement. The argument was not that astronomical explanations were impossible, but that the reported behaviour did not appear to match the observers’ descriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

Modern assessments of historical UFO reports often highlight this same difficulty: eyewitness descriptions are valuable evidence of what people experienced, but human perception is not always reliable for estimating distance, speed, size, or motion in the sky. The National Archives notes that many UFO reports eventually receive explanations involving ordinary objects such as aircraft, balloons, planets, and other atmospheric or visual effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[The National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

### Aircraft and military activity

Asmara’s strategic importance during the post-war period made military activity another possible line of investigation. Eritrea was a strategically located territory, and aviation activity in the wider region was not unusual.

The records show that witnesses and officials considered whether military units near Asmara might have been responsible. However, the surviving correspondence does not identify a confirmed aircraft or military operation that explains the reported objects. The absence of a confirmed source does not prove an extraordinary origin; it simply means the available documentation does not resolve the question.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/the-truth-is-in-here-ufos-at-the-national-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Truth is in Here: UFOs at The National Archives &#124; The National ArchivesOctober 8, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 8, 2009</span></span></span>

## Why the case remains unresolved

### The evidence is strong historically but weak scientifically

The Asmara sighting is unusual among UFO reports because it left a documentary trail. Researchers can examine witness statements and official responses rather than relying only on later retellings. This makes it one of Eritrea’s most notable UFO-related historical cases.

However, the evidence does not meet the standards normally required to identify an unknown aerial phenomenon today. Important missing elements include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Photographs or film recordings.
* Radar or instrument readings.
* Exact measurements of altitude, speed, or trajectory.
* Physical evidence recovered from the event.
* Independent modern observations using calibrated equipment.

</div>

Without these forms of evidence, investigators cannot reliably determine what the witnesses observed.

### The unresolved label does not mean “alien”

A major source of confusion in UFO discussions is the difference between “unidentified” and “extraterrestrial”. In official investigations, an unidentified object means that observers or investigators could not confidently match it to a known explanation at the time. It does not mean that the object was proven to come from outside Earth. The National Archives’ guidance on UFO records reflects this distinction, noting that historical investigations often contained reports that were later explained by ordinary causes while some remained unresolved because available information was insufficient.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[The National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The Asmara case fits this category. It remains unresolved because the evidence is incomplete, not because the surviving documents demonstrate an extraordinary origin.

    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

## Introduction

The Belgian Air Force scramble on the night of **30–31 March 1990** is the single most important military episode of the Belgian UFO wave. It is also the source of some of the strongest—and most disputed—claims that modern military radar tracked an object performing impossible manoeuvres. What unquestionably happened is that Belgian F-16 fighters were launched after ground radar operators and multiple witnesses reported unusual aerial activity. What remains disputed is whether the radar data demonstrated an extraordinary craft or reflected intermittent, ambiguous radar returns that were later overinterpreted.

The distinction matters. The scramble produced genuine military records rather than second-hand stories, yet the most dramatic claims about extreme acceleration and impossible flight have been challenged by later technical reviews. The event therefore illustrates both the value and the limitations of radar evidence in the wider Belgian UFO case.

## Why the fighters were launched

The scramble occurred near the end of the most active phase of [Belgium]({{ 'what-really-happened-during-belgiums-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s 1989–1990 UFO wave. During the evening of 30 March, civilians and police officers around the Wavre and Ramillies areas reported unusual lights. At the same time, military radar operators at the NATO Control and Reporting Centre (CRC) at Glons and personnel at Semmerzake detected intermittent unidentified radar returns that did not correspond to a known aircraft transponder signal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-be-salmon-radar-report-rapport-lt-col-salmon-f16-radar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl be salmon radar report rapport lt col salmon f16 radar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyLt. Around midnight, two armed F-16s were scrambled from Beauvechain Air Base and directed towards the reported targets. This decision is significant because it demonstrates that the military considered the situation operationally unusual, not because it had already identified an extraordinary object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-be-salmon-radar-report-rapport-lt-col-salmon-f16-radar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl be salmon radar report rapport lt col salmon f16 radar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyLt. Unlike many later retellings, the official sequence did not involve pilots visually pursuing a clearly observed triangular craft. The aircraft were launched to investigate uncertain radar contacts whose nature was still unknown.

## What the pilots and radar systems recorded

The interception lasted roughly an hour. During that period the F-16s attempted multiple radar interceptions while receiving guidance from ground controllers.

The radar data also differed from what investigators would normally hope to obtain during an interception. Rather than one uninterrupted track showing a single aircraft's position and motion over time, the evidence consisted of a series of short-lived contacts that repeatedly appeared and disappeared. This distinction became central to later debate.

## Where the dramatic performance figures came from

The Belgian Air Force's public briefing highlighted several radar sequences that appeared remarkable. During some brief lock-ons, calculations suggested rapid changes in speed and altitude, including accelerations from a few hundred kilometres per hour to well above 1,000 km/h within seconds and equally abrupt altitude changes. These figures quickly became the most famous technical evidence associated with the Belgian wave.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-be-f16-radar-charts-1990-f16radarnx1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl be f16 radar charts 1990 f16radarnx1</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyBelgian F-16 Radar Trace Charts, March 30–31, 1990 Eupen/Wavre Interception (part 2), Belgian Air Force / Force Aérienne...</span></span></span>

Supporters of the UFO hypothesis argued that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* multiple radar systems detected something unusual;
* onboard fighter radar independently acquired targets;
* the apparent flight profile exceeded known aircraft performance;
* the repeated loss of lock suggested deliberate evasive manoeuvres rather than ordinary aircraft behaviour.

</div>

These claims gave the Belgian case unusual credibility compared with many UFO reports that relied solely on eyewitness testimony.

However, the extraordinary manoeuvres were never observed directly by the pilots. They were derived from very short radar measurements that depended on the radar maintaining an accurate track throughout each brief contact.

## Why the extreme-speed claim remains unproven

Subsequent technical analysis substantially altered how many researchers interpret the radar evidence.

A detailed engineering review commissioned within the Belgian Air Force concluded that the apparent extreme manoeuvres could be reproduced by characteristics of the F-16's radar tracking system itself rather than requiring an object capable of impossible acceleration. The analysis focused on how the radar's tracking filters behaved when trying to maintain lock on weak, unstable or intermittent returns. Small tracking errors could generate calculated jumps in speed and altitude far greater than any real target motion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-be-salmon-radar-report-rapport-lt-col-salmon-f16-radar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl be salmon radar report rapport lt col salmon f16 radar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyLt. Col. Salmon F-16 Radar Trace Report, Night of 30–31 March 1990, Belgian Air Force (Force Aérienne Belge) · 1990 · UFO...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 1990</span></span></span>

This explanation addresses one of the central misconceptions about the case. The reported accelerations were not direct measurements of an object's physical movement. They were mathematical outputs produced by the radar tracking system after processing imperfect returns. If the radar briefly shifted between different reflectors or partially lost the target, the derived flight path could become unrealistic.

The engineering review therefore argued that the dramatic figures should not be treated as proof that a craft actually executed those manoeuvres.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-be-salmon-radar-report-rapport-lt-col-salmon-f16-radar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl be salmon radar report rapport lt col salmon f16 radar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyLt. Salmon F-16 Radar Trace Report, Night of 30–31 March 1990, Belgian Air Force (Force Aérienne Belge) · 1990 · UFO...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 1990</span></span></span>

Importantly, this does not automatically explain every unidentified radar return recorded that night. It instead narrows the claim: while something may have generated intermittent detections, the radar evidence does not reliably establish extraordinary flight performance.

## What remains unexplained—and what does not

The March 1990 scramble leaves several facts that are generally accepted:

* Belgian authorities genuinely launched interceptor aircraft.
* Ground radar operators reported intermittent unidentified returns.
* F-16 radars briefly obtained several target locks.
* Pilots never visually identified the target.

## How the scramble fits into the wider Belgian wave

The F-16 interception remains the Belgian UFO wave's strongest military episode because it combines eyewitness testimony, operational military response and preserved technical records. At the same time, it demonstrates why sensor evidence must be interpreted cautiously.

The event is often summarised as proof that fighter aircraft chased an object performing impossible manoeuvres. That is not supported by the surviving record. A more accurate description is that Belgian fighters investigated unusual reports, obtained several brief and disputed radar contacts, failed to achieve visual identification, and produced data that later generated both extraordinary claims and equally substantial technical criticism.

For the broader study of the Belgian wave, the scramble is therefore less significant as evidence of an exotic craft than as a case study in how military radar, human observation and later interpretation can diverge. It remains an important chapter in Belgium's UFO history precisely because the records are detailed enough to permit continuing technical debate rather than simple acceptance or dismissal.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });

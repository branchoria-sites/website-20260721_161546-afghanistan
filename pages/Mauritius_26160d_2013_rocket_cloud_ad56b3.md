## Introduction

On the evening of **29 September 2013**, thousands of people across [Mauritius]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-mauritiuss-ufo/' | relative_url }}) watched what appeared to be an enormous glowing orb surrounded by a pale halo drifting across the night sky. Social media quickly filled with photographs and speculation, and many observers wondered whether they were witnessing an unidentified flying object. The event became the island's best-known modern UFO sighting, not because it remained unexplained, but because it was solved unusually well.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Mauritius_26160d_2013_rocket_cloud_ad56b3-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="2013 Sighting illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Mauritius_26160d_2013_rocket_cloud_ad56b3-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Mauritius_26160d_2013_rocket_cloud_ad56b3-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

Within days, astronomers and spaceflight specialists linked the display to the **Falcon 9 v1.1** rocket launched earlier that day by SpaceX from Vandenberg Air Force Base in California. After placing its payload into orbit, the rocket's upper stage performed a routine propellant venting operation. Sunlight striking the expanding cloud at very high altitude created a spectacular luminous halo visible across a huge area of the south-west Indian Ocean and southern Africa. The case is now regarded as one of the clearest examples of how a dramatic astronomical-looking event can produce widespread UFO reports without involving anything extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; SpaceSeptember 30, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

## What witnesses saw across the region

The phenomenon became visible over Mauritius at approximately **9:00 pm local time**. Witnesses described a brilliant white point surrounded by an expanding circular or oval cloud. Many said the object appeared almost motionless before slowly changing shape and fading over several minutes.

## How the Falcon 9 launch matched the sighting

The timing of the event corresponded remarkably well with the maiden flight of the upgraded **Falcon 9 v1.1**, launched from California on **29 September 2013** carrying Canada's CASSIOPE satellite and several secondary payloads.

After satellite deployment, the Falcon 9 upper stage carried out a standard safety procedure by venting its remaining propellant. This prevents pressure from building inside the tanks after the mission is complete.

Astronomers reconstructed the sequence using:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the recorded launch time,
* the rocket's orbital path,
* the expected location of the upper stage,
* witness reports from multiple countries,
* and photographs showing the cloud's changing appearance.

</div>

These independent lines of [evidence]({{ 'evidence-b44fce/' | relative_url }}) all converged on the same explanation: observers were seeing the illuminated propellant cloud produced by the upper stage as it passed over the Indian Ocean roughly fifty minutes after launch.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[space.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; SpaceSeptember 30, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

SpaceX later confirmed that the upper stage had undergone a **controlled propellant venting** procedure, consistent with standard post-mission operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23038-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-explosion-rumors.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Upper Stage of New Falcon 9 Rocket Did Not Explode After Launch, SpaceX Says &#124; Space...</span></span></span>

## Why the cloud looked so extraordinary

The appearance seems surprising only from ground level. In reality, several well-understood physical effects combined to produce an unusually dramatic display.

### Sunlight above the Earth's shadow

Although it was fully dark for observers in Mauritius, the rocket stage was hundreds of kilometres above Earth, where it remained illuminated by the Sun. The expanding gas therefore shone brightly against a dark sky, creating the impression of a glowing object suspended in space.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; SpaceSeptember 30, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

### Rapid expansion in near vacuum

At orbital altitude, released gases encounter extremely thin air. Instead of remaining a narrow exhaust plume, they spread rapidly into a huge cloud hundreds of kilometres across, producing the large circular halo seen in photographs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; SpaceSeptember 30, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

### Illusion of slow motion

Objects in low Earth orbit travel at around **7–8 kilometres per second**, but because they are so distant, their motion across the sky appears surprisingly gentle. The expanding cloud further enhances the illusion that the object is hovering or drifting slowly.

### Bright central point

The brilliant point at the centre was not an alien craft but the rocket stage itself reflecting sunlight while surrounded by its expanding cloud of vented propellant.

## Why many people initially thought it was a UFO

The event possessed several characteristics commonly associated with UFO reports:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* an unfamiliar appearance;
* exceptional brightness;
* silence;
* apparent hovering;
* unusual changes in shape;
* no obvious aircraft navigation lights;
* simultaneous observation by large numbers of people.

</div>

Without knowledge of a rocket launch taking place on another continent less than an hour earlier, the phenomenon naturally appeared mysterious.

The widespread reporting actually strengthened the ordinary explanation. If a relatively low-altitude object had been responsible, its appearance would have differed substantially between locations separated by hundreds or thousands of kilometres. Instead, observers across a vast region described nearly the same phenomenon, exactly as expected for an event occurring in orbit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports (Video) &#124; SpaceSeptember 30, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

## The local investigation and changing explanations

Immediately after the sighting, Mauritian authorities and meteorological services received numerous enquiries. Early suggestions included:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* a meteor,
* a comet,
* an atmospheric phenomenon,
* satellite re-entry,
* or an unidentified flying object.

</div>

Meteorological observations showed no unusual weather conditions capable of producing such a halo. Amateur astronomers, the Astronomical Society of Mauritius and international observers gradually assembled evidence linking the event to the Falcon 9 launch.     section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

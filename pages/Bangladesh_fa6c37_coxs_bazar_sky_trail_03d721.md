## Introduction

On the evening of **8 May 2026**, thousands of people along the beaches of Cox's Bazar watched an extraordinary streak of light cross the sky above the Bay of Bengal. Mobile-phone videos showed what appeared to be a bright, elongated object with a glowing tail, prompting speculation that it might be a meteor, a rocket, or even an unidentified flying object. Within a day, however, the event received a well-supported conventional explanation: the luminous trail was associated with an advanced Indian missile test conducted from Odisha, across the Bay of Bengal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/local-news/zhl20lq825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.prothomalo.com">[Prothomalo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.prothomalo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Missile-shaped flash of light seen in Coxs Bazar sky</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Missile-shaped flash of light seen in Coxs Bazar skyMay 8, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 8, 2026</span></span></span>

For [Bangladesh]({{ 'what-has-really-appeared-over-bangladesh/' | relative_url }})'s modern UFO record, the Cox's Bazar sky trail is significant because it demonstrates how a dramatic and initially mysterious aerial sighting can be explained through publicly available evidence. Rather than remaining an unresolved UFO case, it became an example of how eyewitness reports, regional launch information and atmospheric physics combined to identify the source.

## What witnesses filmed over the Bay of Bengal

The spectacle occurred shortly after sunset, when tourists and local residents noticed a brilliant white-orange object moving rapidly across the western sky above the Bay of Bengal. Videos recorded from Cox's Bazar showed a bright head with a widening luminous plume stretching behind it. The object remained visible for several minutes before fading from view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/local-news/zhl20lq825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.prothomalo.com">[Prothomalo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.prothomalo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Missile-shaped flash of light seen in Coxs Bazar sky</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Missile-shaped flash of light seen in Coxs Bazar skyMay 8, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 8, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why sunlit plumes resemble strange craft

One reason the Cox's Bazar event appeared so extraordinary is that missile exhaust behaves very differently from aircraft contrails.

At high altitude, exhaust gases expand rapidly into extremely thin air, producing a broad cloud rather than a narrow trail. Around sunset, observers on the ground may already be in darkness while the upper atmosphere remains illuminated by direct sunlight. The result is a brilliant white or orange plume glowing against a darkening sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/odisha-missile-launch-mystery-sky-trail-seen-from-bangladesh-after-chandipur-test-2909161" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India Todayagni missile india defence mirv multiple warheads test odisha bangladesh nuclear may 2026 drdo mysterious rocket - India Today...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: may 2026</span></span></span>

Several optical effects make these launches especially dramatic:

* **Rapid expansion:** The exhaust plume grows far larger than the missile itself.
* **Changing colours:** As the Sun sinks lower, the illuminated gases shift from bright white to orange or reddish tones.
* **Twisting appearance:** Winds at different altitudes distort the expanding plume into curved or spiral shapes.
* **Apparent slow movement:** Because the plume occupies a vast volume at high altitude, perspective can make it appear to drift slowly even though the missile itself is travelling at extremely high speed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/odisha-missile-launch-mystery-sky-trail-seen-from-bangladesh-after-chandipur-test-2909161" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India Todayagni missile india defence mirv multiple warheads test odisha bangladesh nuclear may 2026 drdo mysterious rocket - India Today...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: may 2026</span></span></span>

These characteristics have produced similar public reactions around the world after rocket launches and missile tests, particularly during twilight.

## Why this is not considered an unresolved UFO case

From a UFO-investigation perspective, the Cox's Bazar incident differs from genuinely unresolved reports because multiple independent lines of evidence converge on the same explanation.

The key factors include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a precisely matching launch window;
* official confirmation of a missile test;
* consistency between witness videos and known missile-plume behaviour;
* geographical alignment between the launch site and the observed trajectory;
* the absence of contradictory physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/odisha-missile-launch-mystery-sky-trail-seen-from-bangladesh-after-chandipur-test-2909161" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India Todayagni missile india defence mirv multiple warheads test odisha bangladesh nuclear may 2026 drdo mysterious rocket - India Today...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: may 2026</span></span></span>

</div>

Although some social media posts initially speculated about extraterrestrial craft or secret technology before official confirmation, those interpretations lost credibility once the launch was acknowledged and analysed.

Unlike many alleged UFO encounters, the object was not behaving in ways that defied known physics. The unusual appearance resulted primarily from viewing geometry and atmospheric lighting rather than from anomalous flight.

  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>

## Introduction

On 19 November 2023, an unidentified object near Bir Tikendrajit International Airport in Imphal, Manipur, triggered one of [India]({{ 'what-do-indias-best-ufo-cases-really/' | relative_url }})'s most thoroughly documented aviation-related UFO incidents. Unlike many UFO reports that rely solely on witness testimony, this event produced a clear operational response: civil flight operations were suspended for more than three hours, incoming aircraft were diverted, and the Indian Air Force (IAF) launched fighter aircraft to investigate. Those actions establish that authorities regarded the object as a genuine aviation and security concern, even though they did not determine what it was.

At the same time, the incident should not be interpreted as evidence of an extraordinary craft. Publicly available information shows only that something in controlled airspace could not be immediately identified. The military search did not produce publicly released radar data, imagery or recovered material demonstrating an exotic object. The Imphal case therefore illustrates an important distinction in India's UFO record: an unidentified aerial object can justify serious operational measures without proving an extraterrestrial or otherwise extraordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://indianexpress.com/article/north-east-india/manipur/unidentified-flying-object-imphal-flight-operations-disrupted-9033731/lite/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indianexpress.com">[indianexpress.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Indian ExpressNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

## The sighting and the three-hour disruption

The incident unfolded during the afternoon of 19 November 2023 after airport personnel and others reported an unidentified object in the vicinity of Imphal's controlled airspace. According to airport officials, flight operations were suspended at approximately 2:30 pm after reports of the object. The disruption lasted until around 6:00 pm, although witnesses said the object itself was visible for a shorter period before disappearing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://indianexpress.com/article/north-east-india/manipur/unidentified-flying-object-imphal-flight-operations-disrupted-9033731/lite/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indianexpress.com">[The Indian Express]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Indian ExpressNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

* Two inbound commercial flights were diverted.
* Three departing flights were delayed.
* Air traffic control halted departures while awaiting security clearance.
* Normal operations resumed only after competent authorities determined that the immediate risk had passed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://indianexpress.com/article/north-east-india/manipur/unidentified-flying-object-imphal-flight-operations-disrupted-9033731/lite/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indianexpress.com">[indianexpress.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Indian ExpressNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why Rafale fighters were dispatched

The military response has often attracted more attention than the sighting itself.

After civil authorities reported an unidentified object near the airport, the Indian Air Force was informed through established air-defence channels. Defence sources later stated that Rafale fighter aircraft were scrambled from a nearby air base to investigate the reported object. The aircraft conducted low-level searches using onboard sensors but found nothing matching the reported target. A second Rafale sortie was subsequently flown to verify the initial results, again without locating the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/manipur-imphal-airport-shut-after-unidentified-drone-detected-in-airspace-2464935" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">India Today Imphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India TodayImphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace - India TodayNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

This response reflects standard air-defence practice rather than confirmation of an extraordinary event.

When an unidentified object is reported close to an operational airport, particularly in a strategically sensitive region, authorities must initially assume that it could represent one of several possibilities:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* an unidentified drone;
* an unauthorised aircraft;
* an intelligence or surveillance platform;
* another object capable of endangering civil aviation.

</div>

Only after those possibilities are investigated can officials safely conclude that the immediate threat has ended. The decision to launch fighter aircraft therefore demonstrates that authorities considered the report operationally credible enough to investigate—not that they believed it involved alien technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/manipur-imphal-airport-shut-after-unidentified-drone-detected-in-airspace-2464935" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">India Today Imphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India TodayImphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace - India TodayNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

The regional context also mattered. Imphal lies in India's north-east, close to the international border with Myanmar and within a strategically important area where military air surveillance is already taken seriously. During late 2023, Manipur was also experiencing internal security tensions, making unidentified aerial activity particularly sensitive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theprint.in/india/unknown-flying-object-affects-flight-services-in-manipur/1851091/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprint.in">[ThePrint]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprint.in</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on theprint.in.</span></span></span>

## What remained unidentified afterwards

Despite widespread reporting, the public record after the incident remained limited.

No government agency released evidence showing:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* radar tracks identifying the object;
* electro-optical imagery from military aircraft;
* recovered debris;
* sensor data demonstrating unusual flight characteristics; or
* a final investigation identifying a specific cause.

</div>

Likewise, the Rafale sorties reportedly failed to acquire the object either visually or on their onboard sensors. Public reporting therefore ends with the object remaining officially unidentified rather than positively identified as a drone, balloon, aircraft or other phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/manipur-imphal-airport-shut-after-unidentified-drone-detected-in-airspace-2464935" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">India Today Imphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India TodayImphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace - India TodayNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

This distinction is frequently lost in online discussions. "Unidentified" simply means investigators lacked sufficient information to classify the object conclusively. It does not establish that the object possessed extraordinary capabilities or originated from a non-human source.

## Competing explanations

Several explanations have been proposed, but none has been publicly confirmed.

A conventional drone is perhaps the most frequently suggested possibility because small unmanned aircraft have become an increasing concern around airports worldwide. Contemporary reporting also described the object as resembling an unidentified drone in some official discussions. However, no publicly released investigation has confirmed that explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/manipur-imphal-airport-shut-after-unidentified-drone-detected-in-airspace-2464935" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indiatoday.in">[India Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indiatoday.in</span><span class="citation-popover-title">India Today Imphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">India TodayImphal airport shut after unidentified drones detected in airspace - India TodayNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

Other possibilities include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **A balloon or lighter-than-air object.** Atmospheric conditions can cause balloons to appear stationary for prolonged periods before drifting. No official evidence has linked the Imphal object to a balloon.

## Why the Imphal incident matters in India's UFO record

The Imphal airport alert occupies a distinctive place among reported UFO incidents in India because it generated verifiable governmental actions rather than simply anecdotal reports.

Several features make it unusually well documented:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the airport officially acknowledged disruption caused by an unidentified object;
* commercial flights were demonstrably delayed and diverted;
* the Indian Air Force confirmed an operational response through defence sources;
* the event was reported consistently across multiple national news organisations.

</div>

Those facts separate the incident from many historical UFO claims that depend solely on witness recollections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://indianexpress.com/article/north-east-india/manipur/unidentified-flying-object-imphal-flight-operations-disrupted-9033731/lite/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indianexpress.com">[indianexpress.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Indian ExpressNovember 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2023</span></span></span>

However, the same evidence also sets clear limits on what can reasonably be concluded. Within the broader chronology of Indian UFO reports, the Imphal case is therefore best understood as an operationally confirmed unidentified aerial event: authorities genuinely reacted to an object that affected civil aviation, but the available evidence does not establish what the object actually was. That combination of documented disruption and unresolved identification is precisely what makes the incident one of the most significant—and most carefully qualified—entries in India's modern UFO record.

      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

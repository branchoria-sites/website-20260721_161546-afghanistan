## Introduction

[Malta]({{ 'what-really-lies-behind-maltas-ufo/' | relative_url }}) has a small but active community of UFO enthusiasts, yet the country's organised contact movement remains limited in size and has produced no verifiable evidence that its activities establish communication with non-human intelligence. The most visible development has been the emergence of public "Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind" (CE-5) gatherings, where participants combine meditation, group intention and skywatching in the hope of attracting extraterrestrial visitors. These events are noteworthy as a social and cultural phenomenon rather than as scientific evidence. Reported sightings during the sessions have consistently consisted of distant lights, satellites and other ambiguous aerial observations that cannot demonstrate an extraterrestrial origin, and no controlled observations have independently verified contact claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact with aliensJuly 15, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

Unlike countries with long-established civilian UFO organisations that publish investigation reports or maintain extensive sighting databases, Malta's contact scene revolves largely around informal communities, social media and occasional public gatherings. This makes it an interesting case study in how belief, expectation and observational ambiguity interact under real night-sky conditions.

## How organised contact sessions work

The best-documented Maltese contact initiative centres on public CE-5 events organised by Keith Ellul through the Facebook community *Kuntatt – UFO Malta*. Public reporting has described gatherings of several dozen participants meeting at relatively dark coastal locations such as Miġra l-Ferħa, where they spend several hours observing the sky after taking part in guided meditation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact with aliensJuly 15, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

Participants often describe the events as personally meaningful regardless of whether any unusual aerial phenomena appear. However, personal significance is different from objective evidence, and the gatherings themselves do not provide a method for independently confirming extraordinary claims.

## Why reported lights remain inconclusive

The strongest limitation of Maltese contact claims is not necessarily the sincerity of participants but the quality of the observations themselves.

During one documented CE-5 gathering, attendees reported seeing numerous moving lights. By the organisers' own discussions afterwards, many of these objects were readily recognised as satellites. Others described a green light visible in one sector of the sky, but no photographs, triangulated observations, calibrated measurements or independent sensor recordings established what it was.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact with aliensJuly 15, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

This illustrates a recurring problem with contact-based observations:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* distant points of light offer almost no information about size or distance;
* observers frequently interpret movement differently depending on where they are standing;
* expectation may influence which lights attract attention;
* isolated visual impressions cannot distinguish satellites, aircraft, atmospheric effects or genuinely unidentified phenomena.

</div>

Because no objective measurements accompany most reports, the observations cannot establish whether anything unusual occurred beyond normal night-sky activity.

## Satellites, aircraft and night-vision artefacts

Malta's geography creates conditions where ordinary aerial traffic is especially easy to misinterpret.

The islands sit beneath busy Mediterranean air routes, experience frequent satellite passes and have extensive coastal horizons where lights appear against a dark background with few terrestrial reference points. Earlier Maltese UFO reports have already shown that bright planets, meteors and atmospheric effects have repeatedly generated UFO interpretations before later receiving conventional explanations. This wider national pattern provides important context when evaluating contact-session reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.maltastro.org/contact-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maltastro.org">[maltastro.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maltastro.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Contact Us – L-Għaqda Maltija tal-Astronomija</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Contact Us – L-Għaqda Maltija tal-Astronomija</span></span></span>

Several additional factors complicate observations during organised skywatchs:

* **Satellite constellations.** Modern satellite networks produce numerous moving lights that may brighten, dim or disappear as their reflective angles change.
* **Commercial aircraft.** Aircraft approaching or departing Malta can appear stationary for long periods before suddenly changing apparent direction because of perspective.
* **Light pollution.** Malta is among Europe's more light-polluted regions, reducing contrast and making faint objects harder to interpret accurately. Scientific measurements show that only limited parts of the islands retain comparatively dark skies suitable for astronomical observing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04435" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv A photometric mapping of the night sky brightness of the Maltese islands</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A photometric mapping of the night sky brightness of the Maltese islandsFebruary 11, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 11, 2020</span></span></span>
* **Night-vision equipment.** Some enthusiasts use night-vision devices or high-sensitivity cameras that amplify noise, reflections and atmospheric effects alongside genuine celestial objects. These systems increase detection capability but also increase the chance of recording artefacts that require careful technical interpretation.

None of these explanations automatically accounts for every reported light. Instead, they demonstrate that conventional possibilities must be systematically excluded before extraordinary conclusions become justified.

## Why meditation cannot verify extraterrestrial contact

A defining feature of CE-5 is the claim that consciousness itself can initiate communication with extraterrestrial intelligence.

This hypothesis remains outside accepted scientific evidence. No controlled experiment has demonstrated that meditation or collective intention reliably produces measurable aerial phenomena or communication with non-human intelligences. Scientific and sceptical organisations therefore classify CE-5 as an unverified practice whose extraordinary claims have not been supported by reproducible evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2021/08/csi-statement-expresses-concerns-about-ufo-coverage-offers-tips-for-reporters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

That distinction matters because two separate questions are often merged together:

* whether participants genuinely experience unusual or meaningful personal events; and
* whether those experiences demonstrate the presence of extraterrestrial visitors.

The first concerns subjective human experience. The second requires objective physical evidence that has not been produced in Malta.

## What a controlled observation would need

If a Maltese contact gathering were to produce genuinely compelling evidence, it would require documentation well beyond eyewitness testimony or smartphone videos.

Useful scientific documentation would ideally include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* simultaneous recordings from multiple independent cameras positioned at known locations;
* precise timestamps synchronised across all recording devices;
* raw image files with complete metadata preserved;
* independent observers separated geographically to allow triangulation;
* astronomical checks excluding satellites, planets, meteors and known aircraft;
* weather and atmospheric measurements;
* where possible, radar, radio-frequency or other independent sensor data.

</div>

Modern proposals for scientific investigation of unidentified aerial phenomena increasingly emphasise multi-sensor observations because isolated visual reports rarely contain enough information to distinguish genuinely anomalous objects from ordinary ones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18566" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Scientific Investigation of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) Using Multimodal Ground-Based ObservatoriesMay 29, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 29, 2023</span></span></span>

No publicly documented Maltese contact event has yet produced this level of evidence.

## How Malta's contact movement fits within the wider UFO landscape

Malta's organised contact gatherings represent a small but visible strand of the country's broader UFO culture. Unlike historical sighting reports, which concern unexpected observations, contact groups deliberately attempt to create conditions under which an encounter might occur. That makes them culturally distinct but evidentially more demanding.

At present, the available evidence supports several cautious conclusions:

* organised CE-5 gatherings unquestionably take place in Malta;
* participants sincerely report unusual lights and personal experiences;
* many observed objects are openly recognised by attendees themselves as satellites or ordinary aerial traffic;
* no published investigation has demonstrated that a Maltese contact session produced verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial intelligence;
* the claims remain unconfirmed because they rely primarily on subjective interpretation rather than independently corroborated physical data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact with aliensJuly 15, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

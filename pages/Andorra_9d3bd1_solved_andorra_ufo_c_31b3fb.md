## Introduction

The strongest evidence from [Andorra]({{ 'what-really-flew-over-andorra/' | relative_url }})'s UFO record points not towards extraordinary craft but towards ordinary misidentifications and one deliberate deception. Although the principality has generated occasional reports of unusual lights over its mountain valleys, the best-documented incidents have ultimately been explained by identifiable causes: meteorological balloons, Starlink satellite trains, and a carefully staged aerial hoax. These cases are important because they demonstrate how convincing unexplained sightings can appear before sufficient evidence is gathered.

## Meteorological balloons and the 1967 reports

The earliest well-documented solved incidents associated with Andorra date from 1967, when several reports of unidentified aerial objects were eventually traced to meteorological balloons operated by the Spanish weather service.

According to local historical reporting, three balloons descended across the wider Pyrenean region. Two came down in nearby Cerdanya, at Prats and Samsor, while another landed at Anyós in Andorra. Because the balloons had drifted over a broad area, observers at different locations interpreted them as separate unidentified objects rather than as components of a routine meteorological operation.

The significance of the episode lies less in the balloons themselves than in the investigative outcome. Instead of remaining unexplained, the sightings were matched to known atmospheric equipment whose movements were consistent with prevailing winds and the reported observations. The case illustrates a recurring feature of UFO investigations worldwide: high-altitude scientific balloons often appear stationary, unusually bright or oddly shaped because observers lack familiar size and distance references.

Modern atmospheric science continues to rely heavily on weather balloons for upper-air measurements, making them a persistent source of misidentifications in many countries. Their reflective surfaces can produce striking visual effects, particularly around sunrise and sunset when they remain illuminated after the ground has entered darkness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-00482-7" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nature.com">[Nature]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nature.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">High-altitude balloons: a scientists’ guide to what’s up there and why</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">High-altitude balloons: a scientists’ guide to what’s up there and whyFebruary 16, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 16, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why Starlink trains created convincing sightings above Escaldes

One of Andorra's clearest recent solved sightings occurred in February 2023, when residents photographed a procession of bright lights moving across the sky near Escaldes.

To many observers unfamiliar with recently launched satellite constellations, the formation resembled multiple coordinated aircraft or a fleet of unidentified craft. Local astronomical commentary, however, identified the objects as a newly launched Starlink satellite train.

The explanation fits both the timing and the appearance of the observation. Shortly after launch, Starlink satellites travel in closely spaced formations before gradually dispersing into their operational orbits. During twilight they remain illuminated by sunlight even though observers on the ground are already in darkness, producing an apparently silent line of bright moving lights.

This pattern has generated thousands of UFO reports internationally since SpaceX began deploying the constellation. Investigations by France's official UFO investigation body, GEIPAN, have repeatedly classified similar observations as Starlink-related after comparing witness reports with orbital data and launch schedules. In some cases, what witnesses described as a single cigar-shaped craft was shown to be numerous satellites so closely grouped that they blended into one elongated object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/60798" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANPIERRELATTE (26) 22.06.2023 &#124; GEIPANJune 22, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 22, 2023</span></span></span>

For Andorra, the Escaldes sighting demonstrates how improved public awareness of satellite constellations has become an important part of modern UFO identification. What might once have remained unexplained can now often be verified through publicly available orbital information.

## The rectangular-light hoax of 2021

The most dramatic solved Andorran case is not a natural phenomenon but a deliberate fabrication.

Between 9 and 11 November 2021, numerous witnesses across southern France, including observers in Andorra, reported an intensely bright rectangular or oblong object apparently tumbling silently through the night sky. Videos circulated rapidly on social media, and early explanations ranged from drones and satellites to meteors and extraterrestrial craft.

What made the event especially persuasive was its geographical spread. Reports arrived from multiple towns across Occitanie as well as Andorra, giving the impression of independent corroboration. In UFO investigations, multiple witnesses often strengthen confidence that something unusual occurred. In this instance, however, the witnesses had all observed the same manufactured spectacle.

Only days later, French entertainer Rémi Gaillard publicly announced that the phenomenon had been an elaborate practical joke created with collaborators, including personnel associated with a regional weather information service. He released material describing the project and explicitly thanked media organisations for amplifying the mystery before revealing its true origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/ufo-spotted-above-occitanie-is-the-work-of-french-prankster/420321" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: connexionfrance.com">[connexionfrance]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">connexionfrance.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO spotted above Occitanie is the work of French prankster</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO spotted above Occitanie is the work of French pranksterNovember 16, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 16, 2021</span></span></span>

This case differs fundamentally from many UFO reports because it was not merely given a plausible explanation after analysis. The organisers themselves accepted responsibility, turning the incident from an unidentified aerial phenomenon into a documented media hoax.

## What these solved cases reveal about Andorra's UFO record

Taken together, these incidents show that Andorra's strongest documented UFO cases are strongest precisely because they no longer remain unidentified.

Several common mechanisms emerge:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Routine atmospheric technology** can appear extraordinary when viewed at great distance, as demonstrated by the 1967 meteorological balloons.
* **Modern satellite constellations** have introduced a new source of dramatic but predictable sightings, illustrated by the Escaldes Starlink observations.
* **Human deception** can create convincing multi-witness events, as shown by the 2021 rectangular-light hoax.

</div>

Equally important is the quality of the evidence supporting each explanation. The balloon incidents correspond to identifiable meteorological equipment; the Starlink observations match orbital mechanics and have close parallels in official investigations elsewhere; and the 2021 event was resolved through an explicit admission by those who staged it. These explanations therefore rest on substantially firmer evidence than anecdotal claims lacking photographs, corroboration or contemporaneous investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/ufo-spotted-above-occitanie-is-the-work-of-french-prankster/420321" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: connexionfrance.com">[connexionfrance.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">connexionfrance.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO spotted above Occitanie is the work of French prankster</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO spotted above Occitanie is the work of French pranksterNovember 16, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 16, 2021</span></span></span>

## Why solved cases matter

Solved sightings are often more informative than unsolved ones because they reveal the mechanisms that repeatedly generate UFO reports.

For Andorra, the pattern is clear. The country's mountainous terrain offers excellent dark skies but also makes estimating altitude, speed and distance unusually difficult. Bright objects crossing valleys or appearing above ridgelines can seem closer, larger or slower than they actually are. When those viewing conditions combine with uncommon technologies such as high-altitude balloons or newly launched satellite trains, sincere observers may report genuinely puzzling experiences despite there being no extraordinary phenomenon.

These solved cases therefore provide a practical framework for interpreting future reports. They do not prove that every unidentified sighting has a conventional explanation, but they demonstrate that Andorra's best-investigated incidents have consistently moved from mystery to identifiable causes as additional evidence became available.

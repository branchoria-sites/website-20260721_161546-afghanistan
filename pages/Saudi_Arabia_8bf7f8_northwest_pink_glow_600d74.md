## Introduction

The north-west Saudi Arabian pink glow of 2025 is one of the country's most intriguing recent sky mysteries because it combined multiple independent sightings with the absence of a definitive explanation. On two evenings—13 May and 1 July 2025—people across parts of north-west [Saudi Arabia]({{ 'what-really-appears-in-saudi-arabias/' | relative_url }}) reported a bright, circular pink light suspended in the post-sunset sky. Witnesses consistently described a silent, stationary glow that gradually faded after several minutes rather than moving like an aircraft or meteor. Although no official investigation has publicly identified the source, astronomers have proposed several scientifically plausible mechanisms, with high-altitude atmospheric releases and rocket-related phenomena currently regarded as more likely than extraordinary explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

Within the wider record of unusual aerial observations in Saudi Arabia, this event stands out because it was documented close to the time it occurred, photographed by numerous observers and discussed publicly by professional astronomers rather than surviving only as retrospective anecdotal testimony.

## What witnesses reported in May and July 2025

The first known appearance occurred on 13 May 2025. A second, highly similar event followed on the evening of 1 July 2025, prompting wider media coverage because observers recognised that the phenomenon had now repeated within a relatively short period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

* A bright circular or slightly diffuse pink spot appeared after sunset.
* The glow remained almost stationary rather than travelling across the sky.

* After several minutes, the glow slowly diminished instead of ending abruptly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

This combination is significant because it differs from many common misidentifications. Meteors are brief and fast-moving. Aircraft display navigation lights and change position. Lightning is transient. The gradual fading instead suggests a high-altitude illumination process rather than an object emitting its own visible light near the ground.

## Why the appearance was unusual

The pink glow attracted attention largely because of its geometry.

Rather than resembling a streak, flare or expanding fireball, photographs showed an approximately circular luminous patch. Observers also noted its apparent stability, giving the impression that it was suspended in the sky before fading naturally.

Such behaviour is unusual but not unprecedented in atmospheric science. Similar luminous clouds have occasionally been documented elsewhere following rocket launches, upper-atmosphere experiments and high-altitude fuel releases, particularly shortly after sunset when the ground is already dark but sunlight still reaches objects more than 100 kilometres above Earth.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

## Atmospheric releases, rocket plumes and debris

The most detailed public scientific discussion came from Majed Abu Zahrah, head of the Jeddah Astronomical Society.

He emphasised that no confirmed explanation had been established but outlined several scientifically credible possibilities rather than treating the event as an unidentified craft. His principal hypotheses included:

* **High-altitude atmospheric research releases.** Experiments involving vapours such as barium, strontium and ionised oxygen have historically been used to study the ionosphere. When illuminated by sunlight after local sunset, these released materials can produce vivid pink, blue or green glowing clouds visible over large distances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>
* **Rocket exhaust or upper-stage burns.** Rocket engines operating at very high altitude can leave expanding clouds of exhaust illuminated by the Sun even after darkness has fallen on the ground. These "space jellyfish" effects have been documented internationally following orbital launches.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>
* **Satellite or rocket debris.** Residual gases associated with spacecraft or rocket stages could potentially create illuminated clouds if released under suitable lighting conditions. Abu Zahrah also mentioned gases such as helium or hydrogen among the possible contributors.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

These explanations all rely on well-understood atmospheric optics rather than unknown physical mechanisms.

## What evidence supports these explanations?

Several features of the observations fit high-altitude atmospheric phenomena better than conventional low-altitude objects.

First, the glow appeared after sunset, precisely when extremely high-altitude material can remain illuminated by direct sunlight while observers below are already in darkness.

Second, the slow fading is consistent with dispersing gas clouds or exhaust plumes rather than powered flight.

Third, the lack of sound suggests the source, if physical, was extremely distant or at very high altitude.

Finally, the recurrence within roughly seven weeks suggests that the events may have shared a similar mechanism rather than representing a unique one-off anomaly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2606650/saudi-arabia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabnews.com">[Arab News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arab News Mysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab News</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arab NewsMysterious pink glow lights up northwest Saudi skies &#124; Arab NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

None of these characteristics prove a rocket-related origin, but together they make atmospheric and aerospace explanations considerably more plausible than interpretations involving unknown craft.

## Why the case remains unresolved

Despite the plausible hypotheses, no publicly released evidence has conclusively identified what produced either event.

Several important uncertainties remain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* No government agency has publicly attributed the sightings to a specific launch, experiment or aerospace operation.

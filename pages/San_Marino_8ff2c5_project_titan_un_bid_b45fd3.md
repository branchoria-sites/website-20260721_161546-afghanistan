## Introduction

Project Titan is the most significant official step [San Marino]({{ 'is-san-marino-really-a-ufo-hotspot/' | relative_url }}) has taken in relation to unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP), formerly more commonly known as UFOs. Contrary to some headlines, however, the republic did **not** create a United Nations UFO office, nor did the UN adopt a new international UAP programme. What happened was more limited but still noteworthy: in January 2023, San Marino's parliament approved support for a proposal encouraging the government to pursue a UN initiative aimed at establishing a mechanism for periodic international discussion of UAP research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

Within the broader history of San Marino's role in international ufology, Project Titan represents a policy initiative rather than evidence of local UFO incidents. It grew out of decades of UFO conferences hosted in the republic and reflected an effort by advocacy organisations to elevate UAP discussion onto an international diplomatic stage. The parliamentary vote attracted considerable attention because it was presented as the first serious attempt since Grenada's late-1970s initiative to place the subject before the United Nations. Whether that ambition can ever be realised remains unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/news/project-titan-approved" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan Approved...</span></span></span>

## What Project Titan actually proposed

Project Titan was developed jointly by the Italian National UFO Centre (CUN) and the International Coalition for Extraterrestrial Research (ICER), organisations led by Italian researcher Roberto Pinotti. Rather than requesting the UN investigate UFO [sightings]({{ 'sightings-02594f/' | relative_url }}) directly, the proposal envisaged a modest institutional framework inside the existing UN system.

According to the project's published documentation, San Marino would ask the UN Secretary-General to consider creating an office responsible for organising a recurring international conference on UAP research. The proposed conferences would be hosted in San Marino under UN auspices and would bring together government agencies, researchers, scientific organisations and other stakeholders interested in studying unexplained aerial phenomena. Project advocates argued that such a forum could improve transparency and international cooperation while minimising additional bureaucracy by operating within an existing UN structure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/about/project-titan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan...</span></span></span>

The proposal therefore focused on **coordination and information exchange**, not on establishing a global investigative authority or requiring governments to disclose classified material.

## What the 2023 parliamentary vote actually approved

Much of the public confusion stems from misunderstanding what San Marino's parliament voted on.

On 19 January 2023, the Great and General Council approved **Istanza d'Arengo No. 9**, a citizen petition requesting that the Republic participate in Project Titan. Official parliamentary records confirm that the proposal was approved after debate, with a majority voting in favour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

The vote did **not**:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--checklist" markdown="1">

* establish a UN office;
* bind the United Nations to consider the proposal;
* create an international treaty;
* recognise extraterrestrial visitation as fact; or
* commit other UN member states to participate.

</div>

Instead, the vote expressed parliamentary support for San Marino pursuing the initiative diplomatically. During debate, government representatives reportedly described the proposal as potentially beneficial for tourism, media visibility and international profile while indicating general support for exploring the initiative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/news/project-titan-approved" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan Approved...</span></span></span>

In constitutional terms, this was an important domestic political endorsement, but only the beginning of any possible international process.

## Why the proposal attracted international attention

Project Titan received disproportionate attention because it coincided with renewed governmental interest in UAP elsewhere, particularly following Pentagon reporting and increased congressional attention in the United States.

Supporters argued that San Marino was uniquely positioned to act because:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* it is a politically neutral microstate;
* it has hosted international UFO symposia for decades;
* it already possessed relationships with international UFO researchers; and
* a small state can sometimes introduce topics that larger governments avoid for political reasons.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/about/project-titan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan...</span></span></span>

Promoters also highlighted historical precedent by comparing the initiative with Grenada's unsuccessful effort during the late 1970s to encourage UN discussion of UFOs. That comparison helped frame Project Titan as a revival of an idea that had largely disappeared from formal international diplomacy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/news/project-titan-approved" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan Approved...</span></span></span>

## Why the international outcome remains unresolved

The parliamentary approval generated headlines suggesting that UFOs were about to become an official UN agenda item. That interpretation went well beyond what had actually occurred.

Later in 2023, ICER announced that political developments inside San Marino, including a government crisis, prevented implementation of the planned diplomatic initiative during that year's UN General Assembly. Project organisers stated that they hoped the proposal could instead proceed in a future session.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://icer.pt/2023/09/11/project-titan-update/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icer.pt">[icer.pt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icer.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Titan Update &#124; International Coalition for Extraterrestrial Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 11, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 11, 2023</span></span></span>

As matters currently stand:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* no UN office dedicated to UAP has been established;
* no recurring UN conference on UAP exists under Project Titan;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.icerassociation.org/about/project-titan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icerassociation.org">[icerassociation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icerassociation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICE R ASSOCIATION</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICER ASSOCIATION - Project Titan...</span></span></span>
* no General Assembly resolution has adopted the proposal; and
* no evidence indicates that the United Nations has formally accepted the initiative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://icer.pt/2023/09/11/project-titan-update/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: icer.pt">[icer.pt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">icer.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Titan Update &#124; International Coalition for Extraterrestrial Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 11, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 11, 2023</span></span></span>

This distinction is crucial. Parliamentary approval inside San Marino represented **national political support**, not international implementation.

## Separating established facts from advocacy claims

Project Titan illustrates the need to distinguish between documented governmental actions and the expectations of its supporters.

The following points are well supported by [official records]({{ 'official-records-9a9193/' | relative_url }}) and published project documents:

## What Project Titan means in San Marino's UFO history

Within San Marino's broader association with UFO topics, Project Titan is significant because it represents the republic's first formal parliamentary action relating to UAP governance rather than simply hosting conferences or discussions.

It does **not** demonstrate that San Marino possesses unusual UFO evidence, nor does it indicate that the republic has become the centre of an international governmental investigation. Instead, it reflects an attempt by long-established UFO research organisations based around San Marino's annual symposium to move discussion from the conference circuit into an intergovernmental setting.

As a result, Project Titan occupies an unusual place in the country's UFO history. It is best understood as a **policy proposal with symbolic importance**: nationally endorsed, internationally ambitious, but ultimately still awaiting the diplomatic support required to become a functioning United Nations initiative.

## Introduction

Strange lights reported over [Guinea-Bissau]({{ 'what-do-we-really-know-about-guinea/' | relative_url }}) do not automatically indicate an unknown craft. The most documented public case is a 1967 report of a bright spherical object seen by two witnesses, but the available record contains no photographs, radar data, flight [records]({{ 'records-bb3688/' | relative_url }}), or independent investigation that would separate an unusual event from ordinary explanations. The strongest approach is therefore to examine what could create a similar appearance: aircraft lights, satellites, planets and stars, atmospheric effects, and the limits of human perception.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=187616" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 187616February 9, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2025</span></span></span>

## Aircraft and satellite possibilities

A bright moving light is one of the easiest things to misinterpret in the night sky. Aircraft can appear unusual when viewed from the ground because observers often cannot judge distance, altitude, or speed accurately. A plane approaching or turning can seem to slow down, stop, change direction, or suddenly disappear as its lights move away from the observer’s line of sight. At night, navigation lights and landing lights can also make aircraft appear much brighter than their actual size would suggest.

This possibility is relevant to Guinea-Bissau because the country’s limited public reporting infrastructure means that many historical observations cannot easily be checked against aviation records. A witness who sees only a bright point of light may not have enough information to distinguish a distant aircraft from an object performing unusual manoeuvres. The absence of a confirmed aircraft match does not itself prove that an aircraft was not involved; it often reflects missing data.

Satellites create another common source of confusion. A satellite usually appears as a steady point of light moving smoothly across the sky, but its brightness can change rapidly when its reflective surfaces catch sunlight. This can make an object seem to appear, fade, or vanish suddenly. Modern satellite constellations have increased the number of artificial objects visible from Earth, although satellite visibility varies by location, season, orbit, and observation time. Research on satellite visibility shows that large numbers of orbiting objects can affect naked-eye observations of the night sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04328" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visibility Predictions for Near-Future Satellite Megaconstellations: Latitudes near 50 Degrees will Experience the Worst Light Pollu...</span></span></span>

The 1967 Guinea-Bissau report illustrates why these distinctions matter. The witness described a yellow, star-like sphere that appeared to move horizontally, descend, pause, and then accelerate away. Those descriptions are unusual from the observer’s perspective, but the report was made decades after the alleged event and contains no supporting measurements that could establish distance, speed, or trajectory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=187616" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 187616February 9, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2025</span></span></span> A distant aircraft, satellite reflection, or other ordinary object cannot be confirmed from the account alone, but neither can it be ruled out.

## Astronomical and atmospheric causes

### Bright stars and planets can appear to move

Astronomical objects are among the most frequent explanations considered when people report strange lights. Bright planets such as Venus or Jupiter can appear extremely prominent and may be mistaken for nearby objects, especially when the observer has no reference points for distance. In clear, dark skies, bright celestial bodies can appear unusually sharp and isolated.

This factor is particularly relevant in Guinea-Bissau because many areas have relatively dark skies compared with heavily urbanised regions. A bright planet or star can stand out strongly and attract attention. Astronomical visibility also changes throughout the year, meaning a familiar object may appear in an unfamiliar position or at an unexpected time. Sky-mapping tools for Bissau show that bright planets and other celestial objects regularly become visible at different times of night depending on the date and season.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.timeanddate.com/astronomy/night/guinea-bissau/bissau" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timeanddate.com">[Time and Date]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timeanddate.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Time and Date Night Sky Tonight: Visible Planets in Bissau</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Time and DateNight Sky Tonight: Visible Planets in BissauMay 22, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 22, 2026</span></span></span>

The challenge for eyewitnesses is that human vision is poor at estimating the size and distance of isolated lights. A point source thousands of kilometres away and a much closer object can look similar when both appear only as a bright spot. Without angular measurements, photographs with reference points, or multiple observation locations, claims about size and movement remain uncertain.

### Atmospheric effects can create unusual appearances

The atmosphere can also change how ordinary lights appear. Temperature layers, humidity, haze, and turbulence can distort or shimmer light sources, causing them to appear to move, pulse, change colour, or fluctuate in brightness. These effects are particularly noticeable when observing bright objects close to the horizon, where light passes through more of the atmosphere.

Such effects are important when analysing reports of “orbs” or glowing spheres. A witness may accurately describe what they saw — a bright object that seemed to behave strangely — while the cause may involve the interaction between a normal light source and atmospheric conditions. Scientific investigations of unusual aerial observations generally emphasise the need for multiple measurements, including imaging, tracking, and environmental data, because human observation alone often cannot determine an object’s true characteristics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18566" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Scientific Investigation of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) Using Multimodal Ground-Based ObservatoriesMay 29, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 29, 2023</span></span></span>

## Limits of eyewitness interpretation

Eyewitness testimony is valuable because it provides the first record of an event, but it has limitations when describing distant objects in the sky. People are generally good at noticing unexpected lights, yet less reliable at judging speed, size, altitude, and distance without reference points.

The Guinea-Bissau 1967 case demonstrates this problem clearly. The witness reported an object that seemed to move in ways beyond known aircraft capability, but the account provides estimates based on perception rather than instruments. The reported size, speed, and distance could not be independently verified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=187616" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORC UFO Sighting 187616February 9, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2025</span></span></span> This does not make the observation meaningless; it means the evidence supports the existence of a reported sighting, not a confirmed explanation or extraordinary technology.

Several factors can influence interpretation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Lack of scale:** A single light in the sky provides few clues about whether it is nearby or distant.
* **Memory over time:** Reports made many years after an event may preserve important details but can also be affected by later expectations or discussions.
* **Expectation and context:** Historical events, military concerns, or public interest in unusual phenomena can influence how ambiguous observations are interpreted.
* **Missing records:** Without photographs, radar information, aircraft logs, or multiple independent witnesses, many sightings remain unresolved because the evidence is incomplete.

</div>

For Guinea-Bissau, the practical conclusion is that unusual sky reports should be treated as observations requiring investigation rather than immediate proof of unknown technology.     if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

## Introduction

[Liechtenstein]({{ 'what-really-exists-in-liechtensteins-ufo/' | relative_url }}) has never operated a known official UFO investigation programme, yet the country occupies an unusual place in the history of private UFO research because of the reported long-standing interest of Prince Hans-Adam II. Unlike governments that have occasionally studied unidentified aerial phenomena, the prince's involvement appears to have been personal rather than institutional. Evidence gathered from the published diaries of researcher Jacques Vallée, supported by later investigative reporting, indicates that Hans-Adam II privately funded selected UFO-related research projects, maintained relationships with prominent investigators such as Jacques Vallée and Robert Bigelow, and viewed the subject as worthy of serious inquiry. At the same time, there is no evidence that these activities represented the policy of the Principality of Liechtenstein or that they resulted in official government investigations. This distinction is essential to understanding Liechtenstein's place in UFO history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/ita/cultura/famiglie-aristocratiche_il-principe-del-liechtenstein-e-l-ufo-scomparso-verso-la-svizzera/45771102" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swissinfo.ch">[swissinfo.ch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swissinfo.ch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SWI swissinfo.chIl principe del Liechtenstein e l&#x27;UFO scomparso verso la Svizzera - SWI swissinfo.chMay 26, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 26, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why did the prince become interested in UFOs?

The available evidence suggests that Prince Hans-Adam II's interest developed from personal experiences and family stories rather than political or military concerns.

According to Jacques Vallée's published diaries, later corroborated by investigative reporting in the Swiss media, the prince described an unexplained aerial observation from the grounds of Vaduz Castle during his youth. Richard Haines, an aviation psychologist and UFO researcher, reportedly recounted that Hans-Adam had described seeing an unusual object moving towards Switzerland before it disappeared behind trees. In separate diary entries, the prince also reportedly told Vallée that his fascination began after an aunt described seeing a UFO over Munich during the 1950s. Other family members were likewise said to have reported unusual observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com/2017/07/hans-adam-ii-of-liechtenstein-why-was.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com">[blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Scientific Research Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Scientific ResearchUnidentified Anomalous Phenomena - scientific research: Hans-Adam II of Liechtenstein - why was he interested in...</span></span></span>

## What did the prince actually support?

The strongest evidence concerns private financial patronage rather than direct scientific participation.

Jacques Vallée's diaries portray Hans-Adam II as a wealthy benefactor willing to finance researchers whom he regarded as taking anomalous phenomena seriously. Investigative reporting based on those diaries indicates that funding was directed primarily towards American organisations and conferences rather than projects inside Liechtenstein.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/ita/cultura/famiglie-aristocratiche_il-principe-del-liechtenstein-e-l-ufo-scomparso-verso-la-svizzera/45771102" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swissinfo.ch">[SWI swissinfo.ch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swissinfo.ch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SWI swissinfo.chIl principe del Liechtenstein e l&#x27;UFO scomparso verso la Svizzera - SWI swissinfo.chMay 26, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 26, 2020</span></span></span>

* Financial backing for conferences examining alleged alien abduction claims during the early 1990s.
* Donations to organisations including the Fund for UFO Research.
* Support for projects associated with researcher Scott Jones and related foundations studying anomalous phenomena.
* Participation alongside Robert Bigelow in funding research projects examining UFO-related questions and associated reports of anomalous experiences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com/2017/07/hans-adam-ii-of-liechtenstein-why-was.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com">[UFOs Scientific Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Scientific Research Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Scientific ResearchUnidentified Anomalous Phenomena - scientific research: Hans-Adam II of Liechtenstein - why was he interested in...</span></span></span>

## Contacts with Jacques Vallée and Robert Bigelow

Prince Hans-Adam II's significance lies less in conducting research himself than in his connections with influential figures within private UFO studies.

### Jacques Vallée

French computer scientist and UFO researcher Jacques Vallée visited Vaduz Castle and recorded multiple conversations with the prince in his *Forbidden Science* diary series. Those diary entries describe lengthy discussions extending beyond UFO sightings into broader questions involving consciousness, physics and unexplained phenomena. Because Vallée routinely documented meetings shortly after they occurred, historians regard the diaries as valuable primary material concerning relationships within the private UFO research community, even though they naturally reflect Vallée's own perspective.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/ita/cultura/famiglie-aristocratiche_il-principe-del-liechtenstein-e-l-ufo-scomparso-verso-la-svizzera/45771102" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swissinfo.ch">[swissinfo.ch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swissinfo.ch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SWI swissinfo.chIl principe del Liechtenstein e l&#x27;UFO scomparso verso la Svizzera - SWI swissinfo.chMay 26, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 26, 2020</span></span></span>

The diaries also reveal that Hans-Adam II followed developments in international UFO research over many years rather than expressing only a passing curiosity.

### Robert Bigelow

The prince also appears within the network surrounding American entrepreneur Robert Bigelow, one of the most significant private financiers of UFO and anomalous phenomena research during the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries.

Bigelow established the National Institute for Discovery Science (NIDS), a privately funded organisation investigating reports of anomalous aerial phenomena and related subjects. Later reporting suggests Hans-Adam II maintained contact with Bigelow and contributed financially to selected research efforts, although he was not publicly identified as a founder or executive within NIDS itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_for_Discovery_Science" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Institute for Discovery Science</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Institute for Discovery Science</span></span></span>

This association placed the Liechtenstein prince inside an international network of privately funded investigators rather than within any governmental research programme.

## Was this official Liechtenstein policy?

No credible evidence indicates that the prince's interests translated into state policy.

This distinction is often blurred because Hans-Adam II served as head of state while simultaneously acting as a private individual with substantial personal wealth. Available evidence indicates:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* No official Liechtenstein ministry established a UFO investigation office.
* No known military or civil aviation programme was created under royal direction.
* No declassified government archive links the principality to systematic UFO investigations.
* The reported funding came from the prince's personal interests rather than public institutions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans-Adam_II%2C_Prince_of_Liechtenstein" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hans-Adam II, Prince of Liechtenstein</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hans-Adam II, Prince of Liechtenstein</span></span></span>

</div>

Consequently, references to "Liechtenstein funding UFO research" can be misleading if interpreted as describing government policy. A more accurate description is that the country's reigning prince privately supported aspects of international UFO research.

## How reliable is the evidence?

The documentary basis is unusual because much of it originates from personal diaries rather than government files.

Several elements can be regarded as reasonably well supported:

## Introduction

The alleged Lomé beach encounter of 29 March 1974 is by far the most frequently cited UFO case associated with Togo. It is also one of the weakest documented. Although the story has become a staple of UFO literature because of its dramatic claims of paralysis, intense heat, unusual sea behaviour and lingering physical effects, virtually every published version can be traced back to a single anonymous witness interviewed about two years after the event. No known contemporary police report, medical record, photograph, radar data or independently recorded testimony has surfaced to corroborate the account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Togo_30949d_lome_beach_encounter_b2a4b8-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Lome Case illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Togo_30949d_lome_beach_encounter_b2a4b8-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Togo_30949d_lome_beach_encounter_b2a4b8-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

For that reason, the Lomé case occupies an unusual place in Togo's UFO history. It is undeniably the country's defining UFO narrative, yet its significance lies less in the strength of its [evidence]({{ 'evidence-1074c8/' | relative_url }}) than in how a single, delayed testimony evolved into an internationally repeated case. Understanding what was claimed—and what remains unverified—is essential to assessing its historical value.

## What reportedly happened on Lomé Beach in 1974?

According to the account later published by French UFO investigator Joël Mesnard, an anonymous French worker identified only as "A.W." was walking along the Atlantic coast east of Lomé with a Togolese woman during the early hours of 29 March 1974. After spending part of the evening swimming and sitting on the beach, the pair allegedly heard an intense whistling sound coming from the sea shortly before 1:45 am. They then observed what A.W. described as a dark object approaching low over the water before stopping an estimated 200–300 metres offshore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

The witness claimed that the sea suddenly became unusually agitated. A surge of water reportedly advanced much farther inland than expected, forcing him to cling to a coconut tree while supporting his companion. At roughly the same time, three brilliant lights appeared on the object together with additional coloured beams. White light reportedly dominated, although yellow, pink and blue-green illumination was also described.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

One of the most unusual details concerned the surface of the sea beneath the object. A.W. claimed the water appeared depressed into a large circular hollow several metres deep, giving the impression that some force was pushing the ocean downward rather than merely disturbing its surface. This feature later attracted considerable attention within UFO literature because it differed from more conventional reports of hovering craft. However, the observation exists only in the witness's recollection and cannot be independently verified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

The reported event allegedly lasted about twenty minutes before the object departed rapidly over the Atlantic, its lights extinguishing almost simultaneously.

## Witness claims and alleged physical effects

The encounter is often classified by UFO researchers as a "close encounter" because it includes claims of direct physiological effects rather than merely observation of an unusual object.

According to A.W., both witnesses experienced:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* temporary inability to move while illuminated by the lights;
* intense heat despite previously comfortable conditions;
* heavy sweating;
* persistent ringing or auditory discomfort after the object departed.

</div>

He further reported longer-term symptoms during the following days and months, including temporary deafness, headaches, dizziness, fainting episodes, fatigue, anxiety and recurring feelings of suffocation. Mesnard wrote that some of these complaints were still being reported when he interviewed A.W. nearly two years later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

These alleged medical effects are frequently repeated in later UFO books and websites. However, no hospital records, physician's reports or diagnostic examinations have ever been published. Consequently, the claimed health consequences remain anecdotal rather than medically documented.

Equally important, the Togolese woman who allegedly shared the experience has never been publicly identified. No independent interview with her is known, leaving researchers unable to compare two separate witness accounts or determine whether both individuals described the same sequence of events.

## How the story entered the UFO record

A key feature of the Lomé encounter is that it was not publicly documented when it supposedly occurred.

According to Mesnard, A.W. initially remained silent because he feared ridicule and possible official consequences. The account only entered UFO literature after the witness returned to France and contacted researchers. Mesnard published the case in the March 1976 issue of the specialist journal *Phénomènes Spatiaux*, approximately two years after the alleged encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

That publication became the primary source for virtually every later retelling. Modern articles, UFO catalogues, podcasts and internet summaries generally reproduce Mesnard's narrative, sometimes with altered wording, transcription errors or additional speculation. In most cases these later publications are not independent investigations but repetitions of the same original testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

This dependence on a single source significantly limits the evidential weight of the case. Multiple publications describing identical events do not constitute multiple independent witnesses if they all derive from the same interview.

## Which details remain uncertain?

The Lomé case contains several striking claims, but nearly every important element remains difficult or impossible to verify.

The principal uncertainties include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Anonymous primary witness.** A.W.'s identity has never been publicly confirmed, preventing independent assessment of his background or recollection.
* **Missing second testimony.** The Togolese woman has never provided a publicly available account.
* **Delayed reporting.** The approximately two-year interval between the event and the published interview increases the possibility of ordinary memory reconstruction.
* **No contemporary documentation.** No police files, newspaper reports, photographs, weather observations linked to the event or official investigations have been identified.
* **No physical evidence.** The reported sea disturbance, heat and physiological effects left no known measurable record.
* **Transmission through secondary literature.** Later authors generally cite Mesnard rather than new evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/sightings/togo-beach-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Togo Beach UFO Incident – Paralysis, Blinding Lights, And Further Suggestions Of Underwater Bases - UFO InsightJune 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 13, 2021</span></span></span>

</div>

Even relatively minor details illustrate the difficulties of relying on repeated retellings. Some modern summaries contain inconsistent timings or transcription mistakes, demonstrating how secondary accounts can gradually diverge from the original publication.

## How investigators have interpreted the claims

Within UFO research, the Lomé encounter has often been presented as an example of an unusually close interaction between witnesses and an unidentified object. Particular attention has been given to the reported paralysis, intense light, heat and apparent disturbance of the sea surface, all features that some authors compare with other claimed close encounters elsewhere.

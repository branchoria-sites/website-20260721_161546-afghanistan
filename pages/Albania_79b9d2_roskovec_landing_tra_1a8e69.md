## Introduction

The alleged UFO landing at Roskovec on 4 May 2006 remains [Albania]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-albanias-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s best-known case involving claimed **physical traces** rather than distant lights or unidentified objects in the sky. Unlike many Albanian UFO reports, this incident centred on a location that witnesses said could be examined afterwards: dark circular marks on a road, reports of scorched flowers, and claims that an unidentified object briefly touched the ground before departing. Those features have made the Roskovec case a recurring reference point in Albanian media and UFO literature. At the same time, it illustrates the limits of anecdotal evidence. Although photographs of the road markings exist and multiple residents described unusual conditions, there was no documented forensic investigation, no preserved physical samples, and no verified chain of custody for the alleged traces. As a result, Roskovec is best understood as an unresolved landing claim with intriguing but weakly documented physical evidence rather than a confirmed case of an anomalous craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Albania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Albania</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Albania</span></span></span>

## The reported ground encounter

According to the most widely repeated accounts, the incident occurred at approximately 7:00 pm on 4 May 2006 in Roskovec, a town in Fier County. Witnesses described an abrupt environmental change before the alleged landing. They recalled the sky becoming unexpectedly dark despite previously clear weather, a sudden cold wind, bending tree branches and, in some versions, mist or dark smoke. These conditions reportedly lasted only moments before clearing as quickly as they had appeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Albania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Albania</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Albania</span></span></span>

## Why the road markings became the centrepiece of the case

Unlike most Albanian UFO reports, Roskovec produced photographs of an alleged landing site. The images show three dark circular or double-ring marks on an asphalt road. Local photographer Alim Çepele documented the markings shortly after the reported event, and these photographs have since become the defining visual evidence associated with the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Albania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Albania</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Albania</span></span></span>

Supporters of the UFO interpretation argue that the circles were consistent with contact points made by a landed object. They also point to witness claims that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the markings remained visible after the event;
* they appeared unusually dark compared with the surrounding road surface;
* nearby flowers allegedly showed scorching when placed over the affected area; and
* residents believed the surface remained unusually warm for some time afterwards.

</div>

These claims have been repeated in newspapers, television features and later UFO compilations, but important details are missing. No published measurements of the circles are available, no laboratory analysis of the asphalt has been reported, and there are no known contemporaneous temperature readings or chemical examinations confirming abnormal heating or combustion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://alfapress.al/mistere-kuriozitete/raportet-kontaktet-e-ufo-ve-ne-shqiperi-i63483" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: alfapress.al">[alfapress.al]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">alfapress.al</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Raportet - Kontaktet e UFO-ve në Shqipëri - MISTERE&amp;KURIOZITETEMay 16, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2023</span></span></span>

## The missing chain of custody

The greatest weakness in the Roskovec evidence is not the existence of photographs but the absence of documented forensic handling.

A robust physical-evidence investigation would normally attempt to preserve and document:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* exact dimensions of the marks;
* high-resolution photographs taken immediately after discovery;
* independent witness mapping;
* samples of asphalt or soil;
* vegetation samples;
* temperature measurements;
* laboratory testing for hydrocarbons, melting, radiation or unusual chemical residues; and
* secure documentation showing who collected each item and how it was stored.

</div>

No publicly available record indicates that any of these procedures occurred. Instead, the alleged traces entered public discussion largely through witness interviews and media reporting after the event. Consequently, later researchers cannot determine whether the photographed circles changed over time, whether they pre-dated the sighting, or whether they resulted from ordinary road wear or human activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Albania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Albania</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Albania</span></span></span>

The frequently repeated claim that rose petals burned when placed over ash or residue at the site is similarly unsupported by documented testing. No preserved plant samples, photographs of the experiment or laboratory reports have been published.

## Could ordinary causes explain the marks?

The available evidence does not permit a definitive explanation, but several ordinary possibilities remain viable because the physical record is incomplete.

Dark circular asphalt marks can result from:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* vehicle tyre friction or spinning wheels;
* previous road maintenance;
* heat-related changes in bitumen;
* contamination from oils or fuels;
* localised burning unrelated to an aerial event; or
* intentional or accidental human activity occurring before or after the reported sighting.

</div>

Because the site was not isolated or scientifically examined immediately after the event, none of these possibilities can be confidently excluded. Likewise, the environmental descriptions—rapid darkness, wind and apparent atmospheric disturbance—could reflect highly local weather conditions or subjective perception during an unexpected event, although the available evidence is insufficient to determine exactly what occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Albania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Albania</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Albania</span></span></span>

Importantly, sceptical explanations are constrained by the same lack of documentation that limits extraordinary interpretations. The missing evidence prevents either side from conclusively demonstrating what produced the road markings.

## How Roskovec compares with stronger physical-trace investigations

Roskovec is often described as Albania's strongest physical-trace UFO case simply because photographs of alleged landing marks exist. That distinction should not be confused with evidential strength.

Internationally, the most heavily investigated landing-trace cases typically include combinations of:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* documented police involvement;
* multiple independent photographic surveys;
* soil chemistry;
* botanical analysis;
* radiation measurements;
* laboratory examination of trace materials;
* long-term monitoring of vegetation; and
* complete investigative reports.

</div>

Roskovec possesses almost none of these elements in publicly accessible form. Its evidential value therefore rests mainly on the convergence of witness testimony and photographs rather than on independently verified physical science. The case occupies an intermediate position between a simple eyewitness sighting and a properly investigated trace event.

## What a modern reinvestigation would require

Nearly two decades after the incident, opportunities for recovering original physical evidence are extremely limited. Nevertheless, some historical questions could still be addressed through systematic documentation.

Useful avenues for reinvestigation would include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* locating the highest-resolution original negatives or digital files of the road photographs;
* identifying the precise landing location for comparison with historical imagery;
* interviewing surviving witnesses separately using modern cognitive interview methods;
* determining whether municipal road maintenance records mention repairs or existing damage;
* establishing when the photographed circles first appeared and how long they remained visible; and

## Introduction

On 2 June 2018, people across central [Botswana]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-botswanas-ufo/' | relative_url }}) witnessed a brilliant fireball streak across the daytime sky. Unlike most spectacular aerial sightings, this event did not remain unexplained. Within hours, astronomers had linked the fireball to the small near-Earth asteroid **2018 LA**, which had been detected in space roughly eight hours before it entered Earth's atmosphere. Subsequent field investigations recovered meteorites from Botswana's Central Kalahari, creating one of the most complete evidential chains ever assembled for a natural impact event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ub.bw/news/ub-researcher-part-asteroid-fragment-recovery-team" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ub.bw">[UB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ub.bw</span><span class="citation-popover-title">researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of Botswana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UB researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of BotswanaJune 23, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2018</span></span></span>

## The fireball seen over central Botswana

Shortly after midday on 2 June 2018, a bright object entered Earth's atmosphere above southern Africa and produced an intense fireball visible over much of Botswana and neighbouring regions. Witnesses described a brilliant streak followed by fragmentation as the object broke apart under enormous atmospheric pressure. Security cameras in towns including Maun and Rakops also recorded the event, providing valuable visual evidence beyond human testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ub.bw/news/ub-researcher-part-asteroid-fragment-recovery-team" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ub.bw">[UB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ub.bw</span><span class="citation-popover-title">researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of Botswana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UB researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of BotswanaJune 23, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2018</span></span></span>

From the perspective of an eyewitness, the phenomenon could easily have been mistaken for something unusual. The object travelled rapidly, became exceptionally bright and fragmented high in the atmosphere. Such characteristics are common to large meteoroids entering Earth's atmosphere but are also the kinds of observations that, without supporting evidence, can generate speculation about unidentified aerial objects.

What distinguishes this case is that investigators were able to compare eyewitness accounts with astronomical predictions, video recordings and recovered physical material. The event therefore moved rapidly from being an unidentified aerial sight to a conclusively identified meteorite fall.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611328/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PMC7611328</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PMCThe impact and recovery of asteroid 2018 LA - PMCApril 23, 2021</span></span></span>

## Tracking asteroid 2018 LA before impact

The most remarkable feature of this event is that the object was discovered before it struck Earth.

Asteroid 2018 LA was detected by the Catalina Sky Survey in Arizona approximately eight hours before atmospheric entry. Astronomers quickly calculated that its orbit intersected Earth and alerted planetary defence networks. Although the asteroid was only about 1.5 metres across—far too small to survive intact—it became only the third asteroid ever discovered before impact and only the second for which meteorites were later recovered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ub.bw/news/ub-researcher-part-asteroid-fragment-recovery-team" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ub.bw">[ub.bw]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ub.bw</span><span class="citation-popover-title">researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of Botswana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UB researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of BotswanaJune 23, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2018</span></span></span>

Because astronomers already knew the incoming object's trajectory, investigators entered the fireball investigation with unusually strong prior information. Instead of working solely from eyewitness descriptions, they could compare:

* telescope observations made before atmospheric entry;
* predicted impact trajectory;
* fireball videos recorded from multiple locations;
* atmospheric modelling of fragmentation;
* the eventual locations of recovered meteorites.

## How investigators located the meteorites

After the fireball, researchers from Botswana and several international institutions worked together to predict where surviving fragments might have landed.

Scientists combined orbital calculations with security-camera footage to reconstruct the fireball's flight path and estimate the "strewn field"—the area where meteorite fragments were expected to fall after slowing during their final descent. Local knowledge proved essential, with researchers from the Botswana Geoscience Institute, Botswana International University of Science and Technology, the University of Botswana and international collaborators participating in the search.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ub.bw/news/ub-researcher-part-asteroid-fragment-recovery-team" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ub.bw">[UB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ub.bw</span><span class="citation-popover-title">researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of Botswana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UB researcher part of asteroid fragment recovery team &#124; University of BotswanaJune 23, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 23, 2018</span></span></span>

The first meteorite was recovered on 23 June 2018 in the Central Kalahari Game Reserve after several days of systematic searching. It was named **Motopi Pan**, after a nearby watering place. Further expeditions eventually recovered 23 meteorites from the strewn field.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611328/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PMC7611328</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PMCThe impact and recovery of asteroid 2018 LA - PMCApril 23, 2021</span></span></span>

The recovery was scientifically important because fresh meteorites weather rapidly once exposed on the ground. Finding the fragments within weeks preserved valuable chemical and mineralogical information about the original asteroid.

## The evidential chain that solved the case

Few atmospheric events have been documented through as many independent lines of evidence as the 2018 Botswana fireball.

The investigation linked every stage of the asteroid's journey:

1. **Discovery in space** before impact by astronomical surveys.
2. **Accurate orbital calculations** predicting atmospheric entry.
3. **Independent eyewitness observations** across Botswana.
4. **Video recordings** allowing precise trajectory reconstruction.
5. **Atmospheric modelling** predicting the meteorite fall zone.
6. **Recovery of physical meteorites** from the predicted area.


<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Botswana_180c89_asteroid_2018_la_641f2f-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="2018 Fireball illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Botswana_180c89_asteroid_2018_la_641f2f-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Botswana_180c89_asteroid_2018_la_641f2f-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

7. **Laboratory analysis** confirming the recovered rocks originated from asteroid 2018 LA. [PMC+2Wiley Online Library](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611328/)

This chain is especially valuable because each stage corroborates the others. Eyewitness reports alone can be incomplete or mistaken, but in this case they were supported by physical measurements before, during and after atmospheric entry.

For researchers studying unidentified aerial phenomena, the Botswana fireball illustrates how a spectacular visual event can become completely identified when sufficient observational evidence exists.

## What the recovered meteorites revealed

Laboratory analysis showed that the recovered fragments belonged to a rare **HED polymict breccia**, a class of meteorites associated with the differentiated asteroid **4 Vesta**, one of the largest bodies in the asteroid belt. Researchers concluded that 2018 LA was probably excavated from Vesta or a closely related asteroid family before eventually evolving into an Earth-crossing orbit. [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/maps.13653)

The study also estimated that before impact the asteroid measured about 1.56 metres across, possessed a relatively high density and struck Earth's atmosphere with an energy of roughly 0.2 kilotons of TNT equivalent. By combining orbital calculations with geochemical dating, scientists reconstructed not only the asteroid's final hours but also aspects of its geological history stretching back billions of years. [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/maps.13653)

This combination of astronomical observation and laboratory science transformed a brief atmospheric event into one of the best-characterised small asteroid impacts on record.

## Why this matters in Botswana's UFO record

Within Botswana's catalogue of unusual aerial reports, the 2018 fireball occupies a unique position because it is the country's best-documented spectacular sky event—and one that has a definitive explanation.

Unlike anecdotal UFO sightings that depend largely on personal testimony, the 2018 event is supported by multiple independent datasets, including telescope observations, orbital mechanics, camera recordings, meteorite recovery and peer-reviewed scientific analysis. Every major component of the investigation points to the same conclusion: the fireball was produced by asteroid 2018 LA entering Earth's atmosphere. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611328/)

The case therefore serves as an important reference point for evaluating other unusual aerial claims in Botswana. It demonstrates that dramatic visual phenomena are not, by themselves, evidence of unidentified craft. When sufficient observational data exist, even an extraordinary-looking event can often be traced to a well-understood natural cause.

Within the broader chronology of Botswana's reported UFO incidents, the 2018 fireball stands not as an unresolved mystery but as the strongest example of how rigorous investigation, international scientific collaboration and recovered physical evidence can conclusively identify an event that initially appeared extraordinary.       .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();

## Introduction

Recent reports of unusual lights over [Saint Vincent and the Grenadines]({{ 'saint-vincent-and-the-grenadines/' | relative_url }}) have demonstrated how modern spaceflight can generate convincing but entirely conventional “UFO” sightings. Two of the country's best-documented recent cases were not mysterious aircraft or unexplained aerial phenomena, but highly visible SpaceX activities: a train of newly launched Starlink satellites observed in February 2023 and a striking light associated with a Starship test flight crossing the wider Caribbean in November 2024. Together, these incidents illustrate how rapidly changing space technology can outpace public familiarity, especially in regions where rocket launches are not an everyday sight. Rather than adding evidence for unexplained phenomena, both events show how launch schedules, orbital tracking and flight data can resolve sightings that initially appear extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stvincenttimes.com/starlink-satellites-spotted-over-svg-southern-caribbean-islands/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stvincenttimes.com">[St Vincent Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stvincenttimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">St Vincent TimesStarlink Satellites Spotted over SVG, Southern Caribbean IslandsMarch 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2023</span></span></span>

## The Starlink train seen in February 2023

On the evening of 28 February 2023, residents across Saint Vincent and the Grenadines reported an unusual line of bright lights moving silently across the sky. Similar observations came from neighbouring Caribbean islands, including Barbados, Grenada and Trinidad and Tobago, indicating that the display covered a wide geographical area rather than being a localised event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stvincenttimes.com/starlink-satellites-spotted-over-svg-southern-caribbean-islands/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stvincenttimes.com">[St Vincent Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stvincenttimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">St Vincent TimesStarlink Satellites Spotted over SVG, Southern Caribbean IslandsMarch 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2023</span></span></span>

The explanation proved straightforward. Observers were watching a recently launched group of SpaceX Starlink satellites travelling in what is commonly called a **Starlink train**. After deployment from a Falcon 9 rocket, dozens of satellites initially occupy nearly the same orbit. Before they gradually spread apart and raise themselves into operational positions, they can appear from the ground as a remarkably straight procession of evenly spaced lights.

Several characteristics distinguish a Starlink train from reports of unexplained aerial objects:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the lights maintain nearly identical spacing;
* they move at a constant speed without abrupt manoeuvres;
* no flashing navigation lights or engine sounds are present;
* the procession gradually fades as the satellites enter Earth's shadow rather than suddenly accelerating or changing direction.

</div>

These features matched eyewitness descriptions from Saint Vincent and the wider southern Caribbean. Local reporting correctly identified the phenomenon as Starlink satellites shortly after the sighting, and no credible evidence suggested anything beyond a routine satellite deployment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stvincenttimes.com/starlink-satellites-spotted-over-svg-southern-caribbean-islands/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stvincenttimes.com">[St Vincent Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stvincenttimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">St Vincent TimesStarlink Satellites Spotted over SVG, Southern Caribbean IslandsMarch 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2023</span></span></span>

## The bright Caribbean light of November 2024

Another wave of discussion followed in November 2024 when an unusually bright object and luminous plume were observed across parts of the Caribbean during a SpaceX Starship flight.

Unlike the February event, this was not a line of satellites. Instead, the spectacle resulted from the launch and flight of **Starship Flight 6**, which lifted off from Starbase, Texas, on 19 November 2024. During the mission, Starship followed an easterly trajectory over the Gulf of Mexico and Caribbean before continuing towards a planned splashdown in the Indian Ocean. The spacecraft also successfully completed an in-space Raptor engine restart before re-entering Earth's atmosphere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Sixth Flight Test...</span></span></span>

High-altitude rocket flights can produce dramatic visual effects over enormous distances. Depending on the observer's position relative to the Sun, the exhaust plume may remain brightly illuminated even though the ground below is in darkness. This creates the appearance of an enormous glowing object, expanding cloud or brilliant moving light that bears little resemblance to familiar aircraft.

For Caribbean observers, several factors combined to make the launch especially striking:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the vehicle travelled at very high altitude;
* expanding exhaust reflected sunlight long after sunset for many observers;
* the illuminated plume occupied a large apparent area in the sky;
* most residents had little prior experience of seeing Starship flights.

</div>

Because these optical effects are unfamiliar, social media speculation briefly included suggestions ranging from meteors to unidentified flying objects. However, the timing corresponded precisely with SpaceX's published launch schedule and flight profile, and the observed direction matched the rocket's planned trajectory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Sixth Flight Test...</span></span></span>

## Why spacecraft and exhaust look unfamiliar at night

Both the 2023 and 2024 cases illustrate a broader point about modern aerospace activity.

Spacecraft often appear unlike conventional aircraft because they operate under entirely different lighting conditions. While people on the ground may already be in darkness, satellites and rocket exhaust hundreds of kilometres above Earth can still receive direct sunlight. This difference creates several effects that regularly surprise first-time observers.

Starlink satellites are especially conspicuous shortly after launch because they remain close together while reflecting sunlight from their solar panels. As they disperse into operational orbits over subsequent days and weeks, the distinctive "train" disappears, making later sightings much less dramatic.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/space-exploration/international-space-station/spacex-starlink-satellites-seen-as-wonky-streaks-by-iss-astronaut-photos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Starlink satellites seen as ‘wonky streaks’ by ISS astronaut (photos) &#124; Space</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Starlink satellites seen as ‘wonky streaks’ by ISS astronaut (photos) &#124; Space...</span></span></span>

Rocket launches create even more unusual appearances. Exhaust expands rapidly in the thin upper atmosphere into a broad cloud that reflects sunlight far more efficiently than the rocket itself. To ground observers, this can resemble a glowing jellyfish, comet, or luminous sphere moving silently across the sky. The effect has accompanied launches from several countries for decades, but the increasing frequency of SpaceX missions means these displays are becoming more common over the Caribbean.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spacex.com">[SpaceX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spacex.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space XSpace X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX - Starship&#x27;s Sixth Flight Test...</span></span></span>

## What these sightings mean for Saint Vincent and the Grenadines' UFO record

These two incidents highlight an important trend within the country's small body of publicly documented aerial mysteries.

Unlike the unresolved 2014 [Bequia fireball]({{ 'bequia-fireball/' | relative_url }}), both the February 2023 Starlink sighting and the November 2024 SpaceX-related light have strong, independently verifiable explanations based on published launch information, orbital mechanics and consistent regional observations.     if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>

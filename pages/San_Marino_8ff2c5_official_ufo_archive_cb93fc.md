## Introduction

[San Marino]({{ 'is-san-marino-really-a-ufo-hotspot/' | relative_url }}) occupies an unusual position in modern UFO and unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) discussions. The republic has produced a verifiable parliamentary record concerning [Project Titan]({{ 'project-titan/' | relative_url }}) and international UAP cooperation, yet there is no evidence that it maintains a publicly accessible archive of investigated UFO sightings comparable with those released by countries such as the United Kingdom or the United States. This distinction is important. San Marino's documented official involvement concerns policy and international advocacy rather than the publication of national investigative case files.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

For researchers, the practical consequence is straightforward: there are official parliamentary documents that can be examined, but there is no confirmed government repository of historical UFO investigations, military case files or declassified intelligence records from San Marino itself. Understanding that archive gap helps separate documented institutional activity from speculation about hidden records.

## What official UFO records can actually be verified?

The strongest official documentation relates to San Marino's legislature rather than to any national investigative body.

In January 2023, the Consiglio Grande e Generale (the national parliament) approved an Istanza d'Arengo—a citizen petition—supporting participation in Project Titan. The proposal called for San Marino, as a United Nations member state, to promote an international mechanism for periodic scientific discussion of UAP. The parliamentary website preserves the petition, its procedural history, committee referral and approval, providing an authentic government record of political action on the subject.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

This parliamentary documentation establishes several facts that can be verified:

* the proposal formally reached parliament through a recognised constitutional process;
* parliament approved participation in Project Titan;
* the subject entered the official legislative record rather than remaining solely within private UFO organisations;
* the documentation concerns diplomatic and policy initiatives, not the investigation of domestic [sightings]({{ 'sightings-02594f/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

That distinction is frequently overlooked in popular discussions. A government debating UAP policy does not necessarily imply that it possesses classified UFO case files.

## Where are the investigative files?

Current evidence indicates that no public archive of Sammarinese UFO investigations has been identified.

Unlike several larger countries, San Marino has not published collections comparable to:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Britain's former Ministry of Defence UFO files;
* the United States Air Force's Project Blue Book records;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[archives.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>
* more recent publicly organised UAP collections assembled by the US National Archives under statutory disclosure requirements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[nationalarchives.gov.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

Searches of the republic's official parliamentary archives reveal legislative material, motions, petitions and meeting records, but not an archive devoted to UFO reports, witness statements, military investigations or aviation case files. Likewise, there is no publicly identified civilian agency responsible for collecting and releasing national UAP reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/lavori-consiliari.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lavori Consiliari</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Consiglio Grande e Generale...</span></span></span>

This absence should not automatically be interpreted as evidence that records exist but remain hidden. Several more ordinary explanations are equally plausible:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* San Marino's very small size may never have justified creating a dedicated reporting system.
* Any unusual aerial observations may have been handled through ordinary police or civil administration channels without forming a specialised archive.
* Aviation-related matters affecting the republic often overlap geographically with surrounding Italian authorities because San Marino is entirely enclosed by Italy.

</div>

At present, none of these possibilities can be demonstrated through published official documentation.

## Why the archive gap exists

The lack of accessible records reflects institutional realities more than mystery.

San Marino covers only around 61 square kilometres and has no independent military air force responsible for maintaining decades of aerial surveillance files. Consequently, it lacks the bureaucratic structures that produced extensive UFO archives in countries with large defence establishments during the Cold War.

Equally important, San Marino's international reputation within ufology grew through conferences and diplomacy rather than through domestic investigations. Since the early 1990s, international researchers have met in the republic for conferences organised by the Italian Centro Ufologico Nazionale (CUN), meaning that many documents associated with "San Marino" actually concern events hosted there rather than investigations conducted by the Sammarinese state.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

This helps explain why readers often expect an official archive that has never been shown to exist.

## How to assess claims of hidden or misclassified reports

Claims periodically appear suggesting that San Marino possesses secret UFO files or concealed government investigations. At present, these claims lack publicly verifiable supporting evidence.

A careful assessment should distinguish between several different categories.

**Verified**


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Parliamentary documentation relating to Project Titan.
* Official legislative records discussing UAP policy.
* Government acknowledgement that the proposal was debated and approved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/scheda17178175.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: consigliograndeegenerale.sm">[consigliograndeegenerale.sm]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">consigliograndeegenerale.sm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Istanza d&#x27;Arengo n.9 (02-10-2022) - Per l’adesione di San Marino al piano d’azione denominato “Progetto Titano” elaborato congiuntamente...</span></span></span>

</div>

**Unverified**


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Claims that San Marino maintains classified investigative UFO files.
* Allegations of hidden intelligence archives dealing with extraterrestrial encounters.
* Assertions that substantial declassification has occurred without surviving public documentation.

</div>

**Unsupported**

No authenticated catalogue, inventory or official statement has been located identifying an archive of historical Sammarinese UFO investigations comparable to those released elsewhere. Without documentary evidence, references to "secret San Marino UFO files" remain speculative rather than historical fact.

Researchers should also avoid conflating archives held by Italian civilian UFO organisations with government archives belonging to the Republic of San Marino. ## What the archive gap means for researchers

The absence of an accessible investigative archive has important implications for evaluating claims about San Marino.

First, it limits the ability to reconstruct a national chronology of sightings. Without official case files, researchers cannot reliably determine how many reports were received, which agencies investigated them or what conclusions were reached.

Second, it raises the evidential threshold for extraordinary claims. Assertions about government knowledge or concealed investigations require documentary support that has not yet emerged.

Finally, the archive gap shifts attention toward the records that do exist. The parliamentary proceedings surrounding Project Titan provide genuine evidence of San Marino's role in contemporary UAP policy discussions, whereas claims about historical investigative files remain unsupported by accessible official sources.

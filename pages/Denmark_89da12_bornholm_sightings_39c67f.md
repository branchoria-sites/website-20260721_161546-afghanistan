## Introduction

Bornholm has become one of [Denmark]({{ 'what-do-denmarks-ufo-cases-really-show/' | relative_url }})’s most recognisable locations for UFO reports because its geography creates the exact conditions that often produce difficult-to-identify observations: a dark Baltic horizon, wide sea views, limited background references and heavy air and maritime activity. The island’s most discussed modern case came in March 2017, when three fishermen reported an unusual object over the water between Gudhjem and Christiansø. The incident attracted attention because it involved multiple witnesses at close range, yet it also demonstrated the central problem of maritime UFO cases: even apparently clear observations can be difficult to measure accurately without instruments or independent tracking data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/ufo-mail/ufo-mail-2017/ufo-mail-237/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information UFO-Mail 237</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO InformationUFO-Mail 237 - Skandinavisk UFO InformationApril 23, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 23, 2017</span></span></span>

## The 2017 fishermen report

The best-known Bornholm UFO case began on 25 March 2017, when three trolling fishermen were several nautical miles north of Bornholm between Gudhjem and Christiansø. One of the fishermen, Bill R. Hansen, and two companions reported seeing an object approaching their boat from behind. According to their account, the object moved closer, appeared to stop and remain visible for around 15–20 seconds before disappearing in the direction from which it had come.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/ufo-mail/ufo-mail-2017/ufo-mail-237/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information UFO-Mail 237</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO InformationUFO-Mail 237 - Skandinavisk UFO InformationApril 23, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 23, 2017</span></span></span>

The witnesses estimated that the object was roughly 100 metres from the boat and about 100 metres above the sea. They described it as unusually large, with Hansen later estimating a diameter of approximately 15–25 metres, while also acknowledging that judging size and distance over open water is inherently difficult. SUFOI’s later review of the case highlighted this uncertainty as a key limitation: the witnesses were consistent with each other, but the measurements were based entirely on human perception rather than radar, photography or other technical records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SUFOI_Aarsrapport_2018_web.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span></span></span>

One feature that made the case particularly interesting was the response after the sighting. The fishermen contacted maritime and defence authorities, but no official system detected the object. SUFOI reported that the relevant authorities treated the report seriously and attempted to investigate what information was available, but there was no technical confirmation of the object’s presence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SUFOI_Aarsrapport_2018_web.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span></span></span>

The case remains unresolved in the limited sense that no single explanation has been conclusively demonstrated from the available evidence. However, “unresolved” does not necessarily mean evidence for an extraordinary craft. It means that the surviving information is insufficient to identify the stimulus with confidence. The case illustrates the difference between witness credibility and object identification: reliable people can accurately describe an experience while still being mistaken about what caused it.

## Why maritime observations are unusually difficult

Sightings over the sea have several characteristics that can make ordinary objects appear unusual. On land, observers can compare an object with buildings, trees or roads. Over open water, those reference points disappear. A distant aircraft, helicopter, ship, drone or bright atmospheric phenomenon may appear much closer or larger than it actually is.

Bornholm is especially challenging because it sits in the Baltic Sea away from mainland Denmark, creating long unobstructed viewing distances. The same feature that makes the island attractive for watching the sky also removes many of the visual clues needed for identification. A witness looking over the sea may correctly report movement, direction and brightness while still having little ability to estimate altitude, speed or scale.

The 2017 fishermen report demonstrates this problem clearly. The description of an object approaching a boat creates a strong impression of a close encounter, but distance estimates made at sea are vulnerable to error. Without a fixed reference point, the apparent size of an object can be greatly misjudged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SUFOI_Aarsrapport_2018_web.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO Information SUFOI Årsrapport</span></span></span>

Maritime environments also contain many possible sources of unusual observations:

* **Aircraft over water:** Airliners, military aircraft and helicopters can appear isolated against the sky and may show unusual lighting depending on viewing angle.
* **Ships and navigation lights:** At night, distant vessels can appear detached from the horizon, especially in poor visibility.
* **Satellites and astronomical objects:** Bright objects moving steadily across a dark sky can be mistaken for something closer.
* **Drones and experimental equipment:** Modern aerial technology has increased the number of unfamiliar objects people may encounter.

These factors do not explain every report automatically, but they show why maritime UFO cases often remain uncertain without additional evidence.

## Earlier Bornholm reports show a recurring pattern

The 2017 fishermen case was not the first unusual aerial report connected with Bornholm. Earlier cases from the island show a similar mixture of striking witness accounts and later uncertainty.

A notable example comes from August 1987, when a report was submitted through the Bornholm military structure after a witness in Tejn described a large orange-red object appearing to hover silently. The released account noted that there was naval activity in the wider area, including a NATO maritime exercise and helicopter activity, although investigators did not establish a definite explanation from the available information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/danske-x-filer/del-1/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information Hvad indeholder de danske X-Files?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO InformationHvad indeholder de danske X-Files? - 1. del - Skandinavisk UFO InformationFebruary 1, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 1, 2009</span></span></span>

Another example came in 2009, when a Bornholm observer reported seeing a small bright object in a clear sky and provided a photograph. SUFOI’s photo investigators examined the material and discussed possible explanations rather than treating the image as evidence of an unknown vehicle. The case demonstrates a recurring feature of UFO investigation: photographs can appear persuasive to the observer while still lacking the information needed for reliable identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sufoi.dk/ufo-mail/ufo-mail-2009/ufo-mail-113/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sufoi.dk">[Skandinavisk UFO Information]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sufoi.dk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skandinavisk UFO Information UFO-Mail 113</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skandinavisk UFO InformationUFO-Mail 113 - Skandinavisk UFO InformationNovember 10, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 10, 2009</span></span></span>

These reports show a regional pattern rather than a single continuous mystery. Bornholm’s position in the Baltic makes it a place where unusual observations are more likely to attract attention, but the same geography also makes verification difficult.

## What Bornholm adds to Denmark’s UFO record

Bornholm’s importance within Danish UFO history comes from the way it highlights the relationship between environment and interpretation. The island does not stand out because it has produced confirmed evidence of extraordinary objects; instead, it provides several examples of why unusual sightings occur and why some remain debated.

The 2017 fishermen report is the strongest modern Bornholm example because it combines multiple witnesses, a dramatic description and a serious attempt to report the event through official channels. The island’s cases therefore reveal as much about the challenges of human perception as they do about the objects being observed.

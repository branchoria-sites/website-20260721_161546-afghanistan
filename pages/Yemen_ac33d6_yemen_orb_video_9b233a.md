## Introduction

The so-called **[Yemen]({{ 'what-do-we-really-know-about-yemens-ufos/' | relative_url }}) Orb Video** is the most widely discussed modern unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP) case associated with Yemen. Publicly revealed during a United States congressional hearing in September 2025, the infrared footage was described as showing an MQ-9 Reaper drone tracking a small airborne object off the Yemeni coast on **30 October 2024**, while another MQ-9 launched an AGM-114 Hellfire missile towards it. The object appears to remain visible after the encounter, prompting claims that the missile either missed, glanced off, or failed to destroy its target.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/video-house-ufo-hearing-us-missile-strikes-unidentified-object/?intcid=CNR" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsNewly released video at House UFO hearing appears to show U.S. missile striking and bouncing off orb - CBS NewsSeptember 10, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 10, 2025</span></span></span>

The key point is that **the public video alone cannot establish what actually happened**. The clip lacks the sensor metadata, telemetry, full recording, and official technical assessment needed to determine whether the missile scored a direct hit, whether the apparent interaction is influenced by infrared imaging artefacts, or even what the target itself was. As a result, the incident remains genuinely unidentified while falling well short of proving extraordinary technology.

## How the footage entered the public record

The incident became public not through an official military release but through a congressional hearing on UAP transparency held by the House Oversight Committee in September 2025. During the hearing, Representative Eric Burlison presented the infrared recording, stating that it had been supplied by a whistleblower and dated **30 October 2024**. According to his description:

* an MQ-9 Reaper tracked an unidentified orb-like object off Yemen;

## What can actually be seen frame by frame

The video itself is relatively brief and records events through an infrared targeting sensor rather than conventional daylight imagery.

Several features are visible with reasonable confidence.

First, a bright, compact object is tracked against the sea background while the sensor remains locked onto it.

Second, a rapidly moving bright object—identified during the hearing as a Hellfire missile—enters the frame and converges with the tracked target.

Third, a bright flash or overlap occurs at the apparent point of interaction.

Finally, after the encounter, one bright object remains visible together with additional smaller bright fragments or debris while the camera continues tracking. The surviving object appears to continue moving for several seconds before the sensor zoom changes and tracking continues at a wider field of view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/news-features/revelation-that-mq-9-reapers-are-now-engaging-aerial-targets-comes-from-uap-hearing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Revelation That MQ-9 Reapers Are Now Engaging Aerial Targets Comes From UAP Hearing...</span></span></span>

These observations describe only what is visible on the released recording. They do not by themselves reveal:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* whether the missile physically struck the object;
* whether the surviving bright point is the original object or debris;
* whether apparent fragments belong to the missile, the target or both;
* whether sensor bloom or motion blur alters the apparent geometry.

## Why the footage is difficult to interpret

Infrared military sensors do not behave like ordinary video cameras. They display heat rather than visible colour and frequently exaggerate bright sources through blooming, glare and processing effects. Motion blur, camera stabilisation and changing zoom levels can also alter the apparent behaviour of fast-moving objects.

Independent image analysts have highlighted several complications.

One is **parallax**. Because both the observing drone and the target are moving, apparent changes in direction or speed can arise from changing viewing geometry rather than unusual flight characteristics.

Another is **motion blur**. Fast-moving hot objects often appear substantially larger than their true physical size.

A further complication is **automatic tracking**. The sensor continually adjusts to keep its selected target centred, making it difficult to estimate the true trajectory without the aircraft's own flight data.

These are ordinary limitations of infrared targeting systems rather than signs of manipulation or deception. They simply mean that visual appearance alone is insufficient for reliable physical reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/uap-hearing-new-video-yemen-orb.14427/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Hearing New Video</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Hearing New Video - Yemen Orb &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>

## Did the Hellfire missile really hit the orb?

This remains the central unresolved question.

Three broad interpretations have emerged.

**A direct hit followed by survival.** Supporters of an anomalous interpretation argue that the missile visibly impacts the object, yet the object continues flying afterwards. This reading underpins claims that the target resisted a modern precision weapon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/video-house-ufo-hearing-us-missile-strikes-unidentified-object/?intcid=CNR" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsNewly released video at House UFO hearing appears to show U.S. missile striking and bouncing off orb - CBS NewsSeptember 10, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 10, 2025</span></span></span>

**A glancing interaction or incomplete destruction.** Another possibility is that the missile clipped a lightweight object, damaging it without producing the complete destruction viewers might expect. Small fragments continuing on similar trajectories could be consistent with an object breaking apart while remaining partially intact.

**An apparent collision created by viewing geometry.** Independent analysts have argued that the available frames are compatible with perspective effects, debris travelling on different trajectories and infrared imaging artefacts. Under this interpretation, the video does not conclusively demonstrate either a clean impact or an impossible physical interaction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/uap-hearing-new-video-yemen-orb.14427/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Hearing New Video</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Hearing New Video - Yemen Orb &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>

The publicly released material cannot distinguish conclusively between these possibilities.

## What the missing sensor data prevents us from knowing

The greatest limitation is not what appears in the video but what does not.

Military targeting systems routinely record information that has not been released publicly, including:

* exact range to the target;

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* altitude;
* true airspeed;
* camera angle;
* aircraft position;
* weapon telemetry;
* radar returns;
* infrared calibration data;
* complete recording before and after the published clip.

</div>

Without these measurements, independent investigators cannot reliably calculate:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the target's true size;
* its acceleration;
* whether it performed manoeuvres beyond known aircraft capability;
* the exact geometry of the missile intercept.

</div>

This missing context explains why the incident remains unresolved despite extensive online discussion.

## Could the object have been something ordinary?

Several conventional [explanations]({{ 'explanations-26ac80/' | relative_url }}) remain plausible.

A balloon or lightweight airborne object is frequently proposed because such objects can appear unusually bright on infrared imagery and may behave unpredictably after nearby explosions or partial impacts. Some analysts argue that the post-impact motion resembles debris transitioning into a ballistic fall rather than a powered craft continuing controlled flight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/uap-hearing-new-video-yemen-orb.14427/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Hearing New Video</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Hearing New Video - Yemen Orb &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>

The operational environment also matters. The waters around Yemen in late 2024 were an active combat zone containing surveillance aircraft, drones, missiles and numerous airborne objects associated with military operations. That context increases the number of conventional possibilities while simultaneously making independent verification much more difficult.

At present, none of the ordinary explanations has been confirmed publicly, but neither have they been ruled out.

## Why this incident became unusually significant

Unlike many historic UFO reports that rely primarily on eyewitness testimony, the Yemen Orb case involves imagery apparently recorded by a sophisticated military sensor during an operational mission.

That combination gives the incident unusual prominence for several reasons.


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The footage appears to originate from an active military platform rather than a civilian recording.
* It depicts what was presented as an attempted weapons engagement.
* It was introduced during an official congressional hearing rather than emerging solely through social media.
* It has prompted technical analysis from both proponents of UAP transparency and sceptical image analysts.

</div>

The Yemen Orb Video is best understood as an **unresolved military sensor case**, not as proof of exotic technology.

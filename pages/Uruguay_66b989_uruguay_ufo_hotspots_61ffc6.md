## Introduction

Western [Uruguay]({{ 'what-really-explains-uruguays-ufo/' | relative_url }}) occupies a distinctive place in the country's UFO tradition because it combines several factors that rarely occur together: a long-running concentration of reported unusual lights, exceptionally open rural landscapes, dark night skies, and a local culture that has absorbed these stories into tourism and regional identity. The departments of Paysandú and neighbouring Salto, particularly around the [La Aurora]({{ 'la-aurora/' | relative_url }}) ranch and the Termas de Almirón area, have become the country's best-known UFO hotspot. At the same time, official investigations have repeatedly shown that some of the region's most widely discussed sightings have ordinary explanations, illustrating how genuine mystery, folklore and careful investigation coexist rather than reinforce one another.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Fuerza-Aerea-identifico-que-eran-las-luces-avistadas-en-el-cielo-de-Paysandu-en-febrero-uc852807?plantilla=1685&amp;proceso=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Montevideo PortalFuerza Aérea identificó qué eran las luces avistadas en el cielo de Paysandú en febreroMay 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2, 2023</span></span></span>

Rather than representing a unique concentration of proven unexplained aerial phenomena, western Uruguay demonstrates how geography, repeated observation, media attention and local tradition can create a lasting "hotspot" where unusual lights are noticed, reported and remembered more readily than elsewhere.

## Why western Uruguay became the country's best-known hotspot

The area stretching across western Paysandú and northern Salto dominates discussions of Uruguayan UFOs for several interconnected reasons.

First, the region is sparsely populated, with broad agricultural plains, minimal light pollution outside towns and long uninterrupted horizons. These conditions make distant aircraft, satellites, meteors and atmospheric phenomena easier to observe than in Montevideo or other urban centres.

Second, several influential reports dating from the 1970s centred on the La Aurora ranch near the Daymán River. Whether interpreted as extraordinary events or misunderstood natural phenomena, these reports became reference points for later witnesses, creating a cultural expectation that unusual lights might be seen in the area. Once a location acquires such a reputation, observers are often more likely to report ambiguous experiences instead of dismissing them.

Finally, western Uruguay has developed a modest tourism economy around thermal resorts, rural accommodation and night-sky observation. Visitors spending evenings outdoors naturally increase the number of potential witnesses, particularly during clear summer nights.

## Paysandú, Salto and the national sighting map

Although reports occur throughout Uruguay, western departments consistently occupy a disproportionate place in both media coverage and public memory.

### Paysandú

Paysandú is closely associated with two overlapping traditions.

The first is the historic La Aurora case, located close to the border with Salto. Beginning in the mid-1970s, witnesses reported bright lights, alleged close encounters and unusual aerial activity around the ranch. These stories spread throughout Uruguay and neighbouring Argentina, transforming the property into one of South America's better-known UFO locations. While the extraordinary claims remain unverified, the case established the region's enduring reputation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/El-encanto-el-misterio-y-el-magnetismo-de-La-Aurora-que-aun-convocan-visitantes-uc967850?plantilla=1685&amp;proceso=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on com.uy.</span></span></span>

The second is a series of more recent observations around the Termas de Almirón resort. In February 2023 numerous witnesses reported flashing reddish lights, prompting Uruguay's Air Force investigation commission (CRIDOVNI) to deploy investigators, interview witnesses and analyse photographs, videos and aerospace data. Initial media coverage treated the phenomenon as potentially significant, but the official investigation ultimately concluded that the principal observations corresponded to groups of Starlink satellites viewed under suitable conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Tras-denuncias-Fuerza-Aerea-investiga-luces-intermitentes-en-cielos-de-Paysandu-uc845580?plantilla=1385" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Montevideo Portal¿Son ellos?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canal-M - Montevideo Portal...</span></span></span>

This episode illustrates an important pattern in Uruguay's UFO history: a location may produce genuine eyewitness testimony without the observed object being genuinely unidentified after investigation.

### Salto

Salto shares the La Aurora tradition because the ranch lies close to the departmental boundary, separated from Salto by the Daymán River. Consequently, many visitors associate both departments with the same body of stories.

The area has become linked not only with UFO reports but also with spiritual tourism. Pilgrims visit the Padre Pio grotto established on the property, while other visitors arrive primarily because of the site's paranormal reputation. Over time, religious devotion, rural history and UFO folklore have become intertwined rather than existing as separate attractions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://amotermas.uy/padre-pio/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amotermas.uy">[Amo Termas]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amotermas.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Amo Termas Padre Pío</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amo TermasPadre Pío - Amo TermasJuly 15, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2026</span></span></span>

## How landscape and dark skies shape observation

Western Uruguay offers unusually favourable conditions for seeing objects in the night sky, regardless of whether those objects are ordinary or unusual.

Several environmental characteristics increase visibility:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Flat agricultural terrain provides long sightlines with few mountains or forests blocking the horizon.
* Rural districts experience significantly less artificial lighting than metropolitan Montevideo.
* Clear winter and summer nights frequently offer excellent astronomical visibility.
* Thermal tourism encourages people to remain outdoors after sunset.

</div>

These conditions increase the chances of observing satellites, aircraft navigation lights, meteors, space debris and atmospheric optical effects that urban residents might never notice.

This does not mean western Uruguay produces more unexplained phenomena than elsewhere. Instead, it probably produces more observations of all kinds, creating a larger pool from which genuinely puzzling cases may emerge.

The 2023 Starlink episode demonstrates this clearly. The satellite trains were visible across much of Uruguay, yet the reports became concentrated around Paysandú because numerous people happened to be watching the sky in the same location at the same time. Later reports from other departments confirmed that similar lights had been seen elsewhere during the same period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Fuerza-Aerea-identifico-que-eran-las-luces-avistadas-en-el-cielo-de-Paysandu-en-febrero-uc852807?plantilla=1685&amp;proceso=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Montevideo PortalFuerza Aérea identificó qué eran las luces avistadas en el cielo de Paysandú en febreroMay 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2, 2023</span></span></span>

## Tourism, media and the persistence of local legends

The reputation of western Uruguay has grown through decades of repetition rather than through a continuous stream of dramatic new evidence.

Media reporting has played an important role. When unusual lights appear, national newspapers frequently reference La Aurora, reinforcing the connection between new observations and the region's established folklore. Each new report therefore becomes part of an existing narrative rather than an isolated event.

Tourism has also adapted to this reputation. Rural excursions, thermal resorts and nearby attractions often acknowledge the area's mysterious history because visitors already recognise the name. The appeal is broader than belief in extraterrestrial visitors; many tourists simply enjoy visiting a place associated with one of Uruguay's most enduring modern legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/El-encanto-el-misterio-y-el-magnetismo-de-La-Aurora-que-aun-convocan-visitantes-uc967850?plantilla=1685&amp;proceso=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on com.uy.</span></span></span>

Unlike destinations that promote spectacular paranormal claims, the region increasingly balances curiosity with local history, rural culture and religious heritage, allowing the legend to persist even when individual sightings receive conventional explanations.

## Separating folklore from evidence

Western Uruguay's reputation does not mean that every reported light represents an unexplained phenomenon.

Several categories can be distinguished:

**Supported by multiple witnesses**

* The La Aurora reports generated numerous independent testimonies over many years.
* The February 2023 Paysandú observations involved many witnesses across different locations before investigation began.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Tras-denuncias-Fuerza-Aerea-investiga-luces-intermitentes-en-cielos-de-Paysandu-uc845580?plantilla=1385" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Montevideo Portal¿Son ellos?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canal-M - Montevideo Portal...</span></span></span>

**Officially investigated**

* CRIDOVNI conducted field investigations, witness interviews and comparisons with aerospace information for the 2023 events.
* The principal reported lights were identified as Starlink satellite passages rather than remaining unexplained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Fuerza-Aerea-identifico-que-eran-las-luces-avistadas-en-el-cielo-de-Paysandu-en-febrero-uc852807?plantilla=1685&amp;proceso=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montevideo.com.uy">[Montevideo Portal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montevideo.com.uy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Montevideo PortalFuerza Aérea identificó qué eran las luces avistadas en el cielo de Paysandú en febreroMay 2, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2, 2023</span></span></span>

**Still culturally significant despite uncertainty**

* The broader La Aurora tradition continues to attract attention because many of its historical accounts cannot now be independently verified or reconstructed with modern evidence.

## What western Uruguay reveals about Uruguay's wider UFO culture

The Paysandú–Salto corridor illustrates broader patterns found throughout Uruguay's national UFO history.

Repeated sightings tend to cluster where observers have excellent visibility, where previous reports encourage careful watching and where local institutions take witness testimony seriously enough to investigate. [Official inquiries]({{ 'official-inquiries/' | relative_url }}) frequently eliminate ordinary explanations, but a small minority of reports remain unresolved simply because available evidence is incomplete rather than because an extraordinary explanation has been established.

For that reason, western Uruguay is best understood not as proof of unusual aerial visitors but as the country's most influential meeting point between landscape, observation, official investigation and regional storytelling. Its significance lies in showing how a rural landscape can become both a genuine centre of witness reports and a lasting cultural symbol, even while the strongest documented investigations continue to distinguish carefully between unexplained observations and evidence of extraordinary phenomena.

## Introduction

The alleged UFO incidents over Surabaya in September 1964 occupy a distinctive place in [Indonesia]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-indonesias-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO history because they unfolded during one of the country's most tense military periods: the Indonesia–Malaysia Confrontation (Konfrontasi). Unlike many civilian sightings, these reports describe unidentified aerial objects appearing while Indonesian air-defence forces were already expecting hostile aircraft or reconnaissance missions. According to later accounts, anti-aircraft batteries opened fire, radar operators reportedly tracked unusual targets, and fragments from defensive fire allegedly injured civilians in nearby Sidoarjo. Yet despite the dramatic narrative, the case remains difficult to verify because the detailed descriptions come primarily from retrospective accounts rather than surviving contemporary military records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.merdeka.com/histori/read/7888567/misteri-kemunculan-ufo-di-surabaya-dihujani-tembakan-oleh-meriam-tni-tak-mempan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: merdeka.com">[merdeka.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">merdeka.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on merdeka.com.</span></span></span>

The Surabaya episode therefore illustrates a recurring problem in Indonesian UFO research: a historically significant story supported by influential witnesses but lacking the original operational documentation needed to distinguish extraordinary events from wartime confusion, misidentification or later embellishment.

## Why Surabaya Was Especially Sensitive in 1964

The reported sightings coincided with Operation Dwikora during the Indonesia–Malaysia Confrontation, an undeclared conflict in which Indonesia anticipated British and Malaysian air operations, reconnaissance flights and possible attacks. East Java held important military facilities, including the Juanda Naval Air Base, making the region particularly alert to unidentified aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Straits_Crisis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sunda Straits Crisis</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sunda Straits Crisis</span></span></span>

This military setting matters because it changes how the reports should be interpreted.

Rather than observers casually watching unusual lights, personnel were operating under conditions where:

## What Witnesses Claimed to See

The best-known narrative comes from later writings by Jacob [Salatun]({{ 'salatun/' | relative_url }}), an Indonesian Air Force officer who later became a prominent advocate for UFO research and helped establish Indonesia's space agency, LAPAN. His books transformed the Surabaya reports into one of Indonesia's defining UFO cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_reported_UFO_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">List of reported UFO sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">List of reported UFO sightings</span></span></span>

According to these later accounts, between approximately 18 and 24 September 1964:

## Claims of Gunfire, Radar Contacts and Civilian Injuries

The most unusual aspect of the Surabaya case is not the reported lights themselves but the alleged military response.

Several later sources claim Indonesian anti-aircraft artillery fired repeatedly at the unidentified objects. According to Jacob Salatun's version, the objects altered altitude after incoming fire and escaped without visible damage. Some accounts further state that fragments from anti-aircraft shells fell into populated areas around Sidoarjo, injuring civilians sitting outside during the evening.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://betaufo.id/ufo-muncul-saat-dwikora-surabaya-september-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: betaufo.id">[PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">betaufo.id</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo muncul saat dwikora surabaya september 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEHPemunculan UFO secara massal pada masa Dwikora, September 1964 - PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBAN...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: september 1964</span></span></span>

If accurate, this detail would fit a well-understood military hazard. Anti-aircraft ammunition frequently produces dangerous falling fragments even when no target is hit. The alleged civilian injuries therefore do not independently demonstrate that an unusual aircraft was present; they mainly indicate that air-defence weapons were reportedly fired.

Another recurring claim is that radar detected the objects. However, no publicly available radar logs, technical reports or operational records have been released that allow independent researchers to evaluate those alleged detections. The radar element therefore remains an important but unverified component of the story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://betaufo.id/ufo-muncul-saat-dwikora-surabaya-september-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: betaufo.id">[PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">betaufo.id</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo muncul saat dwikora surabaya september 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEHPemunculan UFO secara massal pada masa Dwikora, September 1964 - PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBAN...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: september 1964</span></span></span>

## The Earlier Juanda Base Incident

Some researchers distinguish the famous September sightings from an earlier report centred on the Juanda Naval Air Base in August 1964.

In this account, naval personnel reportedly encountered a luminous reddish-blue object hovering relatively low above a hangar during cloudy conditions before it disappeared. The testimony was reportedly related years later by Lieutenant S. Badirun and preserved through UFO researchers rather than official military publications.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://betaufo.id/ufo-muncul-di-kompleks-pangkalan-al-juanda-surabaya-agustus-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: betaufo.id">[PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">betaufo.id</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo muncul di kompleks pangkalan al juanda surabaya agustus 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA TERBANG ANEHUFO Muncul di Kompleks Pangkalan AL, Juanda, Surabaya, Agustus 1964 - PERKUMPULAN PENGAMAT BENDA T...</span></span></span>

Although sometimes grouped together with the September incidents, the August report appears to represent a separate event. It demonstrates that unusual aerial reports around Surabaya were not limited to a single evening but also highlights another difficulty: the surviving documentation is retrospective rather than contemporary.

## What Documentation Actually Survives?

The greatest weakness in the Surabaya case is not the absence of witnesses but the absence of primary documentation.

Researchers frequently cite:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Jacob Salatun's later books;
* later UFO magazines and enthusiast publications;
* modern retellings based on those earlier secondary sources.

</div>

By contrast, researchers have struggled to locate:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* original anti-aircraft operational logs;
* authenticated radar records;
* official Indonesian Air Force investigation reports;
* preserved government correspondence;
* contemporary newspaper coverage containing detailed eyewitness statements;
* declassified military files describing the engagement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.merdeka.com/histori/read/7767395/sejarah-penampakan-ufo-di-indonesia-disangka-pesawat-canggih-milik-musuh" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: merdeka.com">[merdeka.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">merdeka.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sejarah Penampakan UFO di Indonesia: Disangka Pesawat Canggih Milik Musuh</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sejarah Penampakan UFO di Indonesia: Disangka Pesawat Canggih Milik Musuh</span></span></span>

</div>

The lack of accessible archival material does not prove the event never occurred. Military records may have been lost, remained classified, or never preserved systematically. However, without those records it is impossible to reconstruct events with the confidence expected in modern historical research.

## Conventional Explanations

Several conventional possibilities deserve consideration because they fit the operational environment of September 1964.

### Wartime misidentification

The Indonesia–Malaysia Confrontation created conditions in which unknown aircraft were expected. Stress, darkness and incomplete information can increase the likelihood that ordinary aerial phenomena are interpreted as hostile or mysterious objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Straits_Crisis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sunda Straits Crisis</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sunda Straits Crisis</span></span></span>

### Secret or misunderstood aircraft

At the time, Indonesian forces feared British or Malaysian reconnaissance flights. Experimental aircraft, distant military operations or aircraft observed under unusual lighting conditions could potentially produce confusing reports, although no specific aircraft has been identified that satisfactorily explains every claimed observation.

### Astronomical or atmospheric phenomena

Individual reports of bright lights can sometimes arise from planets, meteors, temperature inversions or atmospheric optical effects. However, these explanations become less persuasive if multiple independent radar contacts and coordinated military responses actually occurred. Since the radar evidence remains undocumented publicly, this possibility cannot be properly evaluated.

### Narrative expansion over time

The surviving descriptions became increasingly detailed decades after the event. As often happens with historically significant UFO cases, later publications may combine multiple incidents, oral traditions and remembered details into a single dramatic narrative, making it difficult to separate original testimony from later interpretation.

    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });

    });

      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }

        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',

      });

      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;

        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });

    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();

        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
